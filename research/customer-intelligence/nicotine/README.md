# Blowin' Smoke — Nicotine Customer Intelligence

This branch converts qualitative nicotine-vaping reconnaissance into a reusable product-selection, merchandising, recommendation, and inventory-support system.

## Research streams

1. **Disposable Intelligence** — flavor, sweetness, ice, sourness, potency, all-day usability, sensory fatigue, actual repurchase.
2. **Open/Refillable Pod Intelligence** — flavor consistency, pod longevity, ecosystem strength, compatibility, consumable availability, size, ergonomics, battery, cost of ownership.
3. **E-Liquid Intelligence** — flavor profile, sweetness, coil impact, all-day usability, nicotine strength, VG/PG/use-case compatibility.

These streams roll upward into **Blowin' Smoke Nicotine Customer Intelligence**.

## Evidence principle

Do not treat launch hype or a single numeric score as market truth. Weight actual behavior more heavily:

1. Actual personal purchase
2. Weeks/months of habitual use
3. Instinctive reach frequency
4. Replacement-pod / consumable rebuy
5. Long-term coil/pod performance
6. Head-to-head comparison
7. First-session experience
8. Specification sheet / manufacturer description

## Core business objective

Blowin' Smoke should become the **decision layer** between manufacturers and customers:

> Research → classify → corroborate → stock selectively → match product to customer → measure what they rebuy → improve recommendations.

The store should not merely sell devices. If Blowin' Smoke sells a pod ecosystem, Blowin' Smoke should support the replacement consumables that make the ecosystem useful.
