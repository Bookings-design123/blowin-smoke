## High-value customer signals

### Habitual reach outranks novelty
The #1 device is the product he reports instinctively reaching for most often. Treat repeated reach as a stronger satisfaction signal than launch enthusiasm.

### Flavor consistency and pod longevity matter
The Argus G4 Mini is praised for flavor remaining consistent and crisp over time. Xlim pods are described as crisp, flavorful, and long-lasting. The reviewer reports approximately 1,800 puffs on the Xlim Pro 3 pod and expects continued life.

### Consumable availability can veto good hardware
OXVA Nexlim and Vaporesso Lux Q3 are liked but demoted partly because the reviewer has difficulty obtaining replacement pods/coil heads.

**Blowin' Smoke rule:** do not stock a pod platform unless replacement consumables can be supported reliably.

### Ecosystem compatibility creates value
A generation-one Vaporesso XROS pod working in a XROS 3 Mini is explicitly praised. Backward compatibility reduces customer friction and inventory complexity.

### Mini/portable form factors can outperform full-size variants
The full Argus G4 leaves while the G4 Mini stays. Several devices are penalized for excessive size. Track portability separately from raw specifications.

### Battery/capacity can compensate for merely good vapor
VOOPOO Navi remains in use because of battery life and capacity even though the reviewer says it only vapes pretty well.

### Ergonomics matter
Repeated signals include hands-free capability, mouthpiece feel, weight, balance, size, and simply enjoying holding the device.

### Dual-mesh / usable-life perception matters
Vaporesso Vibe SE2 is used in Eco mode and later Power mode; the reviewer perceives this as extending usable pod life substantially. Treat this as reviewer perception pending broader corroboration.

## Retail implication — BS Supported

A pod platform should earn a **BS Supported** designation only if Blowin' Smoke can support the ecosystem, not merely sell the starter device.

Candidate customer-facing fields:
- Replacement pods stocked: yes/no
- Platform longevity: weak/moderate/strong
- Cross-generation compatibility: yes/no/partial
- Consumable availability: poor/fair/good/excellent
- Cost of ownership: $/$$/$$$
- Best for: customer job

## Core business lesson

A pod-system sale is not a one-time hardware transaction. It can create a recurring relationship:

> Device → replacement pods/coils → e-liquid → repeat visit → consumables → eventual hardware upgrade

Inventory purchasing should therefore evaluate **lifetime ecosystem value**, not just starter-device margin.
