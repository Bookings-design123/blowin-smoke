# Blowin' Smoke — Customer Satisfaction Blueprint
## Consolidated Reviews 003–006

## Purpose
Convert real vape-review behavior into product-selection and merchandising intelligence. Do not treat influencer scores as market truth. Weight behavioral evidence, independent corroboration, and repeated use more heavily.

## Strongest Product Signal — Geek Bar Sour Gush

Three converging pieces of evidence now support Geek Bar Sour Gush:

1. Laced Up Vapes calls it an all-time favorite and reports **three actual purchases**.
2. EvergreenWayTV independently gives the flavor **4.5/5** and device **5/5**.
3. In a head-to-head comparison, Laced Up Vapes says Foger Sour Gush is more potent and more sour but still prefers Geek Bar for continuous all-day use.

**Status:** HIGH-CONFIDENCE QUALITATIVE SATISFACTION SIGNAL — CURRENT 2026 MARKET VALIDATION REQUIRED.

This establishes satisfaction, not current sales velocity or market heat.

## Core Customer Requirements

### Flavor recognizability
Customers need to understand what they are tasting. OXBAR Blueberry is immediately identifiable; VMTO creates confusion and rejection.

### Flavor cohesion
Individual notes can all be present and still fail as a composition. Foger Sour Punch's sour and punch characteristics are described as fighting each other.

Track `note_presence`, `flavor_balance`, and `flavor_cohesion` separately.

### Sensory expectation accuracy
Foger Lemon Heads and Foger Sour Gush both trigger complaints about unexpected cooling.

**Rule:** Do not rely solely on manufacturer flavor names. Track and disclose sweetness, ice, sourness, dominant notes, secondary notes, style, and finish.

### All-day usability
Foger Sour Gush is more intense and dramatic, yet Geek Bar wins for everyday use.

Track:
- first-hit impact
- flavor potency
- all-day usability
- sensory-fatigue risk
- usage occasion

### Behavioral evidence
Actual repeat purchase is more valuable than a numeric rating. The strongest evidence in this batch is the reported 3x Geek Bar Sour Gush purchase.

## Sweetness Intelligence

There is no universal ideal sweetness.

- OXBAR Blueberry: ~1/10 and still good.
- Geek Bar Sour Gush: packaging/card ~5/10; strong approval.
- Foger Lemon Heads: clearly sweet and positively received.

**Conclusion:** sweetness should fit flavor family and customer segment, not simply be maximized.

## Cooling Intelligence

Cooling is now a major repository dimension.

- OXBAR Blueberry: effectively zero ice and still positively received.
- Foger Lemon Heads: ~4/10 ice; reviewer says customers should be warned.
- Foger Sour Gush: ~5/10 ice; reviewer repeatedly says it was unexpected.
- Geek Bar Sour Gush: card reportedly says 2/10 cooling, but reviewer perceives more.

**Permanent merchandising principle:** "No Ice" should be a first-class filter.

Maintain both:
- `manufacturer_ice_rating`
- `observed_ice_consensus`

## Natural vs Candy

Both can succeed:
- actual/natural blueberry;
- candy strawberry;
- Lemon Heads candy;
- Gusher/fruit-roll-up profiles.

Execution and segment fit matter more than a universal natural-vs-candy preference.

## Flavor Phase Model

Foger Sour Gush shows that reactions can differ across a single draw:
1. Inhale
2. Transition
3. Exhale
4. Finish/linger

Track each phase where evidence permits.

## Review Confidence Model

Classify evidence by actual exposure:
1. First hit
2. First session
3. 24-hour use
4. 2–3 day use
5. Full-device use
6. Repeat purchase

First-session scores should carry less confidence than full-device evidence or repeat purchasing.

## Hardware Intelligence

Device size is salient: OXBAR ROVOTA is called "ginormous." Visual appeal cannot rescue severe flavor rejection. Vapor output is also noticeable and should be tracked.

Permanent hardware fields:
- size
- portability
- visual appeal
- vapor output
- airflow/power modes
- battery longevity
- e-liquid longevity
- leakage
- burnt-hit behavior
- charging
- display usefulness

## Evidence Hierarchy

1. Actual repeat purchase
2. Actual repeated/full-device use
3. Frequent reach behavior
4. Explicit repurchase intention
5. Direct competitor comparison
6. Specific sensory evidence
7. Numeric score
8. Generic praise

## Permanent Disposable Research Schema

### Flavor
flavor_family; flavor_style; dominant_note; secondary_notes; recognizability; advertised-note accuracy; balance; cohesion; potency; sweetness; cooling; sourness; chemical off-notes; inhale; transition; exhale; finish; persistence; all-day usability; sensory-fatigue risk.

### Hardware
size; portability; visual appeal; vapor output; airflow; power modes; battery life; e-liquid longevity; leakage; burnt-hit behavior; charging; display usefulness.

### Behavior
first-hit reaction; frequent reach; all-day use; repurchase intent; actual repurchase count; abandonment/rejection; comparative preference; usage occasion.

### Evidence Quality
reviewer; preference bias; commercial relationship; review stage; independent corroboration count; current-year relevance; price/value evidence; reliability evidence.

## Product Jobs Emerging

Do not build inventory around one universal "best vape." Products appear to serve different jobs:
- Daily driver
- High-impact/occasion flavor
- Low-sweet/no-ice option
- Candy nostalgia
- Maximum sour
- Natural fruit
- Novelty vape

### Geek Bar Sour Gush
**Job:** daily-driver sour/candy vape.
**Priority:** very high for current-2026 validation.

### Foger Sour Gush
**Job:** high-impact maximum-sour experience.
**Priority:** high; validate fatigue and ice consensus.

### Foger Lemon Heads
**Job:** accurate sweet candy-lemon experience.
**Priority:** high corroboration; disclose cooling.

### OXBAR Blueberry
**Job:** natural blueberry / extremely low-sweet / no-ice niche.
**Priority:** determine segment size.

### Foger Sour Punch
**Job:** sour blend.
**Priority:** moderate; test whether cohesion complaint repeats.

### OXBAR VMTO
**Job:** dark mixed-berry novelty.
**Priority:** high caution; require positive counter-evidence.

## Immediate Research Priorities

**Tier 1 — Geek Bar Sour Gush:** recent 2026 reviews, customer comments, availability, reorder evidence, current formulation, reliability, price/margin.

**Tier 2 — Foger Sour Gush and Lemon Heads:** independent reviews, multi-day use, fatigue, ice consensus, repeat purchase.

**Tier 3 — OXBAR Blueberry, Foger Sour Punch, OXBAR VMTO:** quantify niche demand or corroborate/rebut weaknesses.

## Strategic Conclusion

The repository is beginning to answer a more valuable question than "Which vape scores highest?"

> **Which product best fits the customer's intended use, sensory preferences, and tolerance for fatigue?**

That should become the basis for Blowin' Smoke inventory selection, staff recommendations, website filters, and product descriptions.
