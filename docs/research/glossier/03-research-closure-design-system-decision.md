# Glossier Research Closure — Design System Decision

**Status:** Final research decision

**Research phase:** Current discovery phase

**Decision:** Glossier research is closed

## Research conclusion

The Glossier homepage and Balm Dotcom PDP research established sufficient transferable evidence concerning:

- progressive disclosure;
- information hierarchy;
- stable component grammar;
- product-card architecture;
- PDP purchase architecture;
- option and state synchronization;
- media hierarchy;
- action matched to readiness;
- operational trust;
- contextual recommendations;
- restrained surfaces;
- interaction feedback;
- accessible interaction; and
- user-controlled motion.

This is sufficient design-discipline evidence for the current discovery phase.

## Deliberate boundary

Glossier is a **design-discipline reference**.

It is not:

- Blowin' Smoke's brand identity;
- a visual template;
- a source of copy;
- a source of photography;
- a source of trade dress;
- a source of exact layouts;
- a source of product taxonomy; or
- a reason for all three Blowin' Smoke divisions to look like a beauty retailer.

## Original Blowin' Smoke direction

Blowin' Smoke will adapt high-level experience principles into an original system governed by its own Brand Philosophy and domain requirements.

The exact visual language, component expression, hierarchy, content, interactions, motion, commerce states, and cross-division architecture remain original Blowin' Smoke design, product, and engineering decisions.

Do not retroactively attribute original Blowin' Smoke decisions to Glossier.

## Research status

Glossier research is **CLOSED** for the current discovery phase.

Do not recommend additional Glossier research unless a future concrete implementation question cannot reasonably be resolved from the existing final Design DNA specifications or original Blowin' Smoke judgment.
