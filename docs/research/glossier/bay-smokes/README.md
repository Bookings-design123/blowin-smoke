# Bay Smokes Research

## Purpose

Bay Smokes is being studied as a THCA and hemp-commerce reference for the Blowin' Smoke project.

Unlike Glossier, Bay Smokes is NOT being used as the primary visual-design or UI inspiration.

Glossier research defines much of the broader interface discipline, interaction philosophy, component behavior, progressive disclosure, and premium digital experience we are considering for Blowin' Smoke.

Bay Smokes has a different assignment:

Understand how an established THCA-focused retailer organizes, explains, merchandises, and sells products within this category.

The objective is to extract domain-specific commerce intelligence that can inform the THCA division of Blowin' Smoke.

---

## Research Objectives

Study how Bay Smokes handles:

### Product Taxonomy

Identify how products are organized across categories such as:

- THCA flower
- pre-rolls
- concentrates
- vapes/disposables
- gummies/edibles
- Delta-8 products
- related hemp-derived products

Determine which distinctions matter to customers and how the catalog communicates them.

### Product Discovery

Analyze:

- navigation
- collections
- filtering
- sorting
- search
- featured products
- best sellers
- new products
- promotional merchandising
- product recommendations

Determine how customers move from broad category interest to a specific product.

### Product Information

Identify which information is presented before and after entering a product page.

Study:

- product type
- strain/product name
- quantity
- cannabinoid information
- composition
- potency information
- flavor/aroma descriptions where applicable
- product format
- price
- availability
- variants
- product imagery
- usage or handling information
- warnings/restrictions

### Trust & Product Proof

Study how Bay Smokes attempts to establish confidence through:

- lab reports / COAs
- testing information
- cannabinoid documentation
- product transparency
- reviews
- ratings
- shipping information
- age restrictions
- policies
- educational information
- customer support
- guarantees or service promises

Determine which proof is useful and which elements are merely promotional.

### THCA Education

Study how the site explains:

- THCA
- product categories
- terminology
- differences between formats
- product selection
- legal/regulatory information
- responsible purchasing information

Identify where education reduces customer uncertainty.

### Merchandising

Analyze:

- bundles
- discounts
- promotions
- cross-selling
- related products
- product collections
- featured strains/products
- scarcity messaging
- subscription/replenishment if present

Determine how Bay Smokes increases basket size and product discovery.

### Commerce States

Identify how the interface handles:

- available
- unavailable
- sold out
- sale
- new
- featured
- variants
- Add to Cart
- product configuration
- age/restriction requirements

---

## Blowin' Smoke Application

The purpose of this research is NOT to make Blowin' Smoke look like Bay Smokes.

The purpose is to understand the THCA customer's decision environment.

Useful principles should eventually be combined with:

- Blowin' Smoke's Brand Philosophy
- Glossier-derived UI/UX discipline
- Blowin' Smoke's independent operator identity
- original product curation
- accurate product documentation
- transparent commerce

The THCA side of Blowin' Smoke should ultimately improve upon the category rather than imitate an existing retailer.

---

## What We Are NOT Copying

Do not reproduce Bay Smokes':

- branding
- logo
- copywriting
- photography
- color palette
- typography
- page layouts
- trade dress
- promotional campaigns
- exact navigation
- exact product cards
- exact merchandising language

Do not assume an industry practice is good merely because Bay Smokes uses it.

Research should distinguish between:

WHAT EXISTS

WHY IT EXISTS

WHETHER IT WORKS

WHETHER BLOWIN' SMOKE SHOULD ADAPT, IMPROVE, OR REJECT IT

---

## Deliverable

The final Bay Smokes research should help define the domain-specific requirements for the Blowin' Smoke THCA division.

Every major conclusion should answer:

**What does a THCA customer need to know, understand, verify, or decide—and how can Blowin' Smoke serve that need better?**
