# 1. Domain Conclusion

Smoke Cartel demonstrates that Glass & Accessories commerce is not one homogeneous catalog. It combines complete pieces, functional components, fitted accessories, maintenance goods, consumable tools, electronic hardware, and maker-led objects. The customer is often deciding not merely whether they like a product, but whether it will physically fit, arrive complete, work in the intended orientation, suit the desired material or use, and match the scale they expect.

The homepage is effective at breadth, momentum, recognizable product types, maker visibility, price comparison, ratings, and introductory education. It is less effective at decision safety. Dimensions, joint size, gender/type, angle, included pieces, material, and compatibility are exposed inconsistently—often inside titles, URLs, filenames, or photography rather than a stable field system. A verified buyer explicitly reports that an item was larger than expected, reinforcing the scale problem.

The governing Blowin' Smoke opportunity is not to reproduce Smoke Cartel's storefront. It is to combine:

**Blowin' Smoke identity + Glossier interaction discipline + normalized physical data + verified fit relationships + scale-conscious photography + plain-language education.**

The Glass & Accessories division should feel native to the same three-division store while using a product-type-specific information contract where physical fit demands it.

## Product-role model

Blowin' Smoke should distinguish at least:

1. **Complete piece** — usable as the primary object, subject to included-part requirements.
2. **Fitted component** — bowl, banger, downstem, ash catcher, adapter, or other part whose value depends on a connection relationship.
3. **Replacement component** — restores or replaces a known part of a complete piece.
4. **Universal accessory** — works broadly without a piece-specific fit relationship, where verified.
5. **Maintenance product** — cleaner, plugs/caps, brushes, or care tools.
6. **Session tool** — grinder, lighter, torch, storage, or preparation accessory.
7. **Electronic hardware** — vaporizer/e-rig with technical and potentially replacement lifecycles.
8. **Maker object / collectible piece** — craftsmanship and provenance may be decision-critical alongside function.

This model is an **original Blowin' Smoke recommendation**, not an observed Smoke Cartel taxonomy.

## Taxonomy diagnosis

Smoke Cartel proves the breadth of the domain but not a complete ontology. The same homepage mixes product types, functions, makers, promotional groupings, and technical descriptors. Blowin' Smoke should not encode “Best Seller,” “14mm,” “glass,” or a maker name as peers in one category tree. Those attributes support different customer questions and must remain queryable without becoming competing storefront divisions.

## Card risks

1. **Titles carry incompatible data types.** Product identity, physical dimension, material, feature, and maker can blur together.
2. **Fit facts are inconsistently visible.** A product URL can contain “male,” while the visible title omits it; an image filename can contain 90-degree and 14mm male while the card title does not.
3. **Package contents are visually implied.** A photographed bowl or downstem is not proof that it is included.
4. **Scale is hard to judge.** Isolated product imagery and an explicit buyer size complaint show that attractive photographs are insufficient.
5. **CTA semantics are opaque.** Add to cart may correspond to a resolved SKU, while View product may indicate options or complexity, but that remains **INFERRED**, not established.
6. **Membership price competes with product truth.** Multiple price layers increase cognitive load without improving fit confidence.

## Original Blowin' Smoke card grammar

The shared three-division card should retain stable slots for media, identity, price, availability, and action. Glass & Accessories may add no more than two or three decision-critical facts appropriate to the product role:

- complete piece: height/scale class, material, primary joint;
- fitted component: joint size, gender/type, angle, fit status;
- maintenance/session tool: function, capacity/count, applicable material;
- electronic hardware: key technical role plus compatibility status.

The CTA should be **Add to cart** only when the sellable variant is resolved and no unresolved fit decision is material. Otherwise use a truthful state such as **Select options**, **Check fit**, or **View details**. These labels are candidate language, not production copy.

## Data rules

- Store values as structured attributes, not title fragments.
- Separate nominal connection size from measured overall dimension.
- Store units and support human-readable conversions without inventing precision.
- Distinguish **unknown**, **not applicable**, **not supplied**, and **not verified**.
- Record the evidence source and last verification date for fit-critical data.
- Treat option-dependent attributes as variant data; never let a card combine specifications from different variants.
- Do not use photography, URL slugs, or filenames as the authoritative source of a specification.

# 7. Physical Compatibility Model

## Domain problem established

The capture contains products whose usefulness can depend on joint size, gender/type, angle, clearance, orientation, and included parts. A 14mm bowl, a 45-degree ash catcher, and a piece shown with fitted components are **OBSERVED** examples. Yet no visible fit statement or compatibility relationship is present. Smoke Cartel therefore establishes the customer problem, not a verified solution.

## Original relationship graph

```text
COMPLETE PIECE
  ↕ primary connection
BOWL / BANGER / NAIL
  ↕ intermediate connection and clearance
ASH CATCHER / RECLAIM CATCHER
  ↕ conversion
ADAPTER
  ↕ service relationship
DOWNSTEM / REPLACEMENT COMPONENT
  ↕ care applicability
CLEANER / MAINTENANCE TOOL
```

Relationships should be bidirectional where meaningful. A customer should be able to begin with **“I own this piece”** and retrieve verified compatible parts, or begin with **“I need this bowl/downstem/ash catcher”** and see which pieces or connection specifications it supports.

## Minimum fit states

- **COMPATIBLE** — verified to fit the selected context.
- **INCOMPATIBLE** — a known size, gender/type, angle, clearance, or system conflict exists.
- **CONDITIONALLY COMPATIBLE** — works only with a stated adapter, orientation, measurement, or configuration.
- **UNIVERSAL** — verified to apply broadly within a defined scope, never used as a vague marketing synonym.
- **UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED** — evidence is insufficient; must never silently resolve to compatible.
- **NOT APPLICABLE** — the product role requires no physical fit relationship.

## Relationship facts

A fit record may require:

- source product or specification;
- target product or specification;
- relationship type;
- nominal joint size;
- joint gender/type on both sides;
- angle/orientation;
- clearance or dimensional constraint;
- adapter requirement;
- included-versus-separate status;
- evidence source;
- verification date;
- exception/condition text.

## Commerce consequences

Verified compatibility should eventually influence discovery, search, filters, product cards, PDP facts, recommendations, bundles, action readiness, cart checks, replacement routes, sold-out alternatives, and post-purchase care. The exact implementation remains an original Blowin' Smoke product and engineering decision.

- Three products expose “Load video” markup. **OBSERVED**; playback and content are **NOT DETERMINABLE**.

- No dependable human-scale reference, measurement overlay, or standardized comparison object is exposed. **OBSERVED absence in the capture.**
- One verified buyer says a product was “little bigger than wanted.” **OBSERVED quotation**, reinforcing but not quantifying scale uncertainty.

## Photography jobs for Blowin' Smoke

Every product image should have a defined decision job:

1. **Identity view** — clean overall form.
2. **Scale view** — ruler/measurement graphic, hand/context image where appropriate, or standardized size silhouette.
3. **Connection view** — close-up of joint, threading, seal, opening, or attachment point.
4. **Contents view** — everything included, separated and labeled.
5. **Assembly/orientation view** — how parts connect and which direction they face.
6. **Material/craft view** — thickness, finish, weld/joint quality, texture, or maker detail.
7. **Use-context view** — safe, compliant context that clarifies posture, footprint, and handling without copying competitor art direction.
8. **Variant view** — exact sellable color/form; no cross-variant image ambiguity.

Photography must support text, not replace it. Image captions and alternative text should describe useful visual information without smuggling critical specifications into metadata that sighted customers cannot see.

# 10. Education, Care, and Decision Support

## Observed education

The FAQ explains the broad difference between bongs and dab rigs, recommends beginner categories, describes the bong assortment, and addresses shipping, authenticity, membership, and returns. The welcome section frames curation, gear breadth, discreet shipping, deals, and bundles. **OBSERVED.**

## Original educational ladder

1. **Category level:** What is this product type, what problem does it solve, and when is it appropriate?
2. **Card level:** Which two or three facts determine whether the shopper should open the PDP?
3. **PDP purchase zone:** What fits, what is included, what is required, and what must be measured?
4. **Inline fit help:** Plain-language diagrams for size, gender/type, angle, and measurement.
5. **Post-purchase:** Assembly, safe handling, cleaning, care, and replacement guidance.
6. **Support escalation:** A fit-help path when data is unknown or the customer cannot identify their piece.

Education should reduce errors without profiling or patronizing the customer. This directly supports Article I's **respect over manipulation** and **performance over appearance alone** principles.

# RESEARCH VERDICT

## What did Smoke Cartel establish?

Glass & Accessories commerce requires product-role clarity, physical dimensions, joint/connection data, angle, material, included and replacement pieces, scale-conscious media, maker identity, care guidance, and honest operational states. It also established that broad assortment, recognizable categories, multi-angle media, vendor visibility, ratings, education, and merchandising can help discovery.

## What did it not establish?

The homepage did not verify a compatibility engine, normalized physical schema, measurement convention, package-contents model, replacement graph, search interpretation, recommendation logic, cart validation, responsive interaction, or accessibility behavior beyond surviving structural labels. Those mechanisms must never be attributed to Smoke Cartel.

## What should Blowin' Smoke adapt?

- category and intent-led discovery;
- visible maker/provenance context;
- multi-view product media;
- beginner education and operational clarity;
- popularity and novelty as secondary discovery aids;
- richer product modules when they resolve meaningful decisions.

## What should Blowin' Smoke improve or originate?

- normalized physical schemas;
- bidirectional fit relationships;
- explicit compatibility states;
- measurement and scale guidance;
- included/required/replacement separation;
- role-aware cards and comparisons;
- fit-aware actions, recommendations, bundles, cart checks, alternatives, and support;
- material-aware care;
- original curation and maker storytelling grounded in Article I.

## What must Blowin' Smoke reject?

- Smoke Cartel's branding, logo, trade dress, visuals, photography, copy, layouts, typography, colors, prices, promotions, category order, product cards, membership systems, vendor presentation, and labels;
- promotional pressure that outranks product correctness;
- fit facts hidden in product strings, URLs, filenames, or images;
- photographed components being treated as proof of inclusion;
- popularity or customer reviews being treated as compatibility evidence;
- unknown fit silently treated as compatible.

## Final direction

The Glass & Accessories division should not become a separate store inside Blowin' Smoke. It should use the same premium, restrained, accessible, state-aware commerce foundation as THCA and Vape & Nicotine, while adding the physical-data and fit intelligence this domain demands.

That synthesis supports Blowin' Smoke's constitutional promise: respect the customer's intelligence, provide substance behind the design, preserve independent judgment, and make the customer feel they discovered a better-informed way to shop.
