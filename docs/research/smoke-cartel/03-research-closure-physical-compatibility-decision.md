# Smoke Cartel Research Closure — Physical Compatibility Architecture Decision

**Status:** Final research decision

**Research phase:** Current discovery phase

**Decision:** Smoke Cartel research is closed

## Research conclusion

The Smoke Cartel homepage research is sufficient to establish the Glass & Accessories domain requirements for Blowin' Smoke.

The research established the problem space:

- physical dimensions matter;
- joint size alone is insufficient;
- joint gender/type matters;
- angle and orientation matter;
- clearance and length can matter;
- included, required, optional, and replacement components must be distinguished;
- physical compatibility can determine whether a legitimate product is usable;
- scale must be communicated through both structured measurements and decision-oriented media;
- material affects care and product relationships;
- maker and provenance can be product truth;
- fragile products create distinct operational and support states.

This is sufficient domain evidence for the current discovery phase.

## Deliberate research boundary

Blowin' Smoke will not perform additional Smoke Cartel PDP, collection, interaction, or compatibility research during the current discovery phase.

The absence of a verified Smoke Cartel compatibility system is not a blocking research gap. No unverified compatibility behavior, interface, measurement convention, validation method, recommendation logic, cart check, or support workflow may be represented as observed Smoke Cartel behavior.

Additional Smoke Cartel research should not be recommended unless a future concrete implementation decision reveals a material domain question that cannot reasonably be resolved through:

1. original product judgment;
2. authoritative manufacturer or maker data; or
3. physical validation.

## Original Blowin' Smoke direction

Blowin' Smoke will define an original physical-data and compatibility architecture.

Authoritative implementation truth should eventually come from:

1. manufacturer or maker documentation;
2. verified SKU and package information;
3. physical receiving inspection;
4. standardized Blowin' Smoke measurement procedures; and
5. verified product-pair compatibility testing where necessary.

Competitor titles, URLs, filenames, images, and apparently similar products are not authoritative sources of fit. Information that cannot be verified must remain **UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED** rather than being inferred.

## Compatibility states

The original Blowin' Smoke system should support the following states where applicable:

- **COMPATIBLE** — verified to work in the identified product or specification context.
- **INCOMPATIBLE** — a verified physical conflict prevents correct use.
- **CONDITIONALLY COMPATIBLE** — works only under a stated condition, configuration, measurement, orientation, or adapter requirement.
- **UNIVERSAL** — verified to work throughout a clearly defined scope; never used as an unqualified marketing claim.
- **UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED** — available evidence is insufficient to make a fit determination.
- **NOT APPLICABLE** — the product relationship does not require physical compatibility.

Unknown or unverified information must never be silently treated as compatible.

## Candidate physical relationship model

Where meaningful, compatibility relationships should be bidirectional:

```text
COMPLETE PIECE
↕
BOWL / BANGER / NAIL
↕
ASH CATCHER / RECLAIM CATCHER
↕
ADAPTER
↕
DOWNSTEM / REPLACEMENT COMPONENT
↕
CARE / MAINTENANCE PRODUCT
```

A customer should eventually be able to begin with an owned complete piece and retrieve verified compatible components. A customer should also be able to begin with a component or replacement need and identify the complete pieces, connection specifications, or materials it supports.

The relationship model is a candidate direction, not a claim that every adjacent object in the diagram connects directly or that every product requires compatibility logic.

## Commerce consequence

Verified physical relationships may eventually influence:

- product discovery;
- search;
- filtering;
- product-card information;
- PDP information;
- recommendations;
- bundles;
- Add to Cart readiness;
- cart checks;
- replacement discovery;
- sold-out alternatives;
- care-product guidance;
- post-purchase support.

The exact architecture, interface, measurement standards, validation procedures, recommendations, cart checks, and support workflows remain original Blowin' Smoke product and engineering decisions. This decision does not authorize implementation.

## Evidence discipline

Do not retroactively attribute this architecture to Smoke Cartel.

Smoke Cartel established the customer problem.

Blowin' Smoke is defining the solution.

## Research status

Smoke Cartel research is **CLOSED** for the current discovery phase.

No additional Smoke Cartel research is required unless a future concrete implementation decision reveals a material domain question that cannot reasonably be resolved through original product judgment, authoritative manufacturer or maker data, verified SKU/package data, receiving inspection, standardized measurement, or product-pair validation.
