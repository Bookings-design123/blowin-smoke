# Smoke Cartel Research

## Purpose

Smoke Cartel is being studied as a glass, smoking-accessories, hardware, and physical-compatibility commerce reference for the Blowin' Smoke project.

Smoke Cartel is NOT being used as the primary visual-design or UI inspiration.

Glossier research defines the broader interaction discipline and product-experience system.

Bay Smokes research defines THCA-specific commerce requirements.

VaporDNA research defines vape/nicotine technical-product and compatibility requirements.

Smoke Cartel has a different assignment:

Understand how an established smoke-accessories retailer organizes, explains, merchandises, and sells products whose usefulness depends on physical dimensions, materials, connections, included pieces, function, care, maker, and fit.

The objective is to extract domain-specific commerce intelligence for the Glass & Accessories division of Blowin' Smoke.

## Research Objectives

Study how Smoke Cartel distinguishes:

- bongs / water pipes
- dab rigs
- hand pipes
- bubblers
- grinders
- bowls
- ash catchers
- bangers / nails where present
- vaporizers
- e-rigs
- cleaners
- storage
- torches / lighters
- adapters
- accessories
- replacement pieces
- makers / vendors

Separate:

PRODUCT TYPE

FUNCTION

MATERIAL

SIZE / DIMENSION

CONNECTION / JOINT

ANGLE

COMPATIBILITY

INCLUDED PIECE

REPLACEMENT PIECE

MAKER / BRAND

PROMOTIONAL COLLECTION

Do not collapse these dimensions into one taxonomy.

### Physical Compatibility

This is a critical research objective.

Determine how the commerce system helps customers understand:

- joint size
- joint gender/type where applicable
- connection angle
- bowl/banger fit
- downstem compatibility
- ash-catcher compatibility
- adapter compatibility
- device/accessory fit
- replacement-piece requirements
- universal versus piece-specific accessories

A wrong physical specification can make a product unusable even if it is otherwise correct.

## Blowin' Smoke Application

The purpose of this research is NOT to make Blowin' Smoke look like Smoke Cartel.

The purpose is to understand the decision environment for glass and accessories.

The Glass & Accessories division should eventually combine:

- Blowin' Smoke's Brand Philosophy
- Glossier-derived interface discipline
- normalized physical product data
- compatibility-aware commerce
- accurate dimensions
- clear connection/joint information
- photography that communicates scale and fit
- authentic maker/product information
- original Blowin' Smoke curation

The customer should not need expert smoke-shop knowledge to determine whether two physical components will fit together.

---

## What We Are NOT Copying

Do not reproduce Smoke Cartel's:

- branding
- logo
- copywriting
- photography
- color palette
- typography
- layout
- trade dress
- promotional campaigns
- exact category structure
- exact product cards
- membership mechanics
- pricing
- labels
- vendor presentation

Do not assume an industry practice is good merely because Smoke Cartel uses it.

Research should distinguish:

WHAT EXISTS

WHY IT EXISTS

WHETHER IT WORKS

WHETHER BLOWIN' SMOKE SHOULD ADAPT, IMPROVE, OR REJECT IT

---

## Governing Research Question

Every major conclusion should answer:

What does a glass or accessories customer need to know, measure, compare, verify, or match in order to purchase the correct piece—and how can Blowin' Smoke make that decision easier and harder to get wrong?

---

## Deliverable

The final Smoke Cartel research should define candidate domain requirements for the Blowin' Smoke Glass & Accessories division.

Special emphasis must be placed on:

1. product taxonomy;
2. dimensions;
3. material;
4. joint / connection information;
5. physical compatibility;
6. included and replacement pieces;
7. photography and scale;
8. maker / vendor identity;
9. product education;
10. care and maintenance;
11. availability and variant states;
12. prevention of incompatible purchases.

These requirements must integrate with the existing Blowin' Smoke system rather than create a disconnected third store.
