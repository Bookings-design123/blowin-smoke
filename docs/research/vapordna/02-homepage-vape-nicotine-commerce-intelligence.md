## Source-integrity boundary

The VaporDNA capture contains 265 lines and 10,288 bytes. Substantive homepage content occupies lines 1–192; blocked-resource artifacts begin at line 193. # 18. Recommended Next Research Target

## COMPATIBILITY / REPLACEMENT EXPERIENCE

### Why this target should be next

Compatibility is the distinctive risk of vape/device commerce and the largest unresolved homepage question. Product cards show technical products and model language but no evidence that devices, pods, coils, tanks, cartridges, accessories, and replacements are connected. A conventional device PDP could reveal one product's facts, but a complete compatibility/replacement journey tests whether the retailer turns those facts into a customer safeguard.

The target should begin with one currently sold refillable device or tank, identify its platform and included component, follow every visible route to a replacement pod/coil/accessory, then reverse the path from the component back to supported devices. This is a page type/experience target, not authorization to browse or interact yet.

### What questions it would answer

- Is compatibility explicit, structured, and bidirectional?
- Which identifiers define a platform or fit relationship?
- Are required, included, optional, and replacement components distinguished?
- Can a customer find parts from an owned device and devices from a part?
- Are incompatible, conditionally compatible, universal, and unknown states represented?
- Do recommendations, search, filters, cards, PDPs, and cart preserve the same relationship?
- How do stock, variants, successor models, and sold-out alternatives affect fit?
- Does the system explain replacement cycles and setup without requiring expert knowledge?

### What we should extract

1. device/platform identifiers and aliases;
2. relationship types and directionality;
3. included/required/optional/replacement classification;
4. compatibility statements and evidence/source ownership;
5. device-first and component-first discovery routes;
6. product-card and PDP fit indicators;
7. option, stock, and CTA state under compatible/incompatible/unknown conditions;

# RESEARCH VERDICT

## 1. What did VaporDNA teach us that Glossier could not?

VaporDNA exposes the domain structure of vape retail: complete devices, disposables, refillable hardware, e-liquid, nicotine pouches, technical accessories, model families, and specifications such as puff count, capacity, material, battery, charging, and coil language. It reveals that a product may be legally purchasable and in stock yet still be wrong because it does not fit, requires another component, or belongs to a different lifecycle. Glossier supplied state discipline; it could not supply this device/consumable/replacement ontology.

## 2. What did VaporDNA teach us that Bay Smokes could not?

Bay Smokes centers composition, format, laboratory proof, eligibility, and batch/product trust. VaporDNA introduces a many-to-many technical ecosystem: devices connect to pods, coils, tanks, liquids, accessories, and replacement parts. It also exposes a combined device-consumable object—the disposable—beside refillable systems where hardware and replenishment separate. That creates compatibility, included-component, maintenance, replacement, and reverse-lookup requirements that THCA commerce does not share in the same form.

## 3. What existing Glossier-derived principles should remain unchanged?

- stable product-card and PDP grammar;
- progressive disclosure;
- one dominant job per surface;
- selected-option, price, media, facts, stock, eligibility, and CTA synchronization;
- action matched to readiness;
- operational truth before promotional persuasion;
- proof layered before social proof and cross-sell;
- contextual recommendations with an explicit reason;
- persistent orientation and strong known-item search;
- accessible interaction and equivalent non-hover access;
- restrained, controlled motion;
- one shared Blowin' Smoke system with division-specific evidence slots.

Vape compatibility extends the shared state model; it does not replace it.

## 4. What VaporDNA practices should Blowin' Smoke reject?

- using overloaded product names as the specification schema;
- inconsistent technical facts across comparable cards;
- exposing battery, charging, coil, strength, or flavor data only in image metadata;
- presenting technical products without visible compatibility or replacement relationships;
- allowing promotion, percentage savings, or free-product mechanics to outrank fit and safety;
- generic Shop Now actions that hide option or compatibility readiness;
- sold-out dead ends without explained recovery;
- treating a 21+ gate as complete eligibility or safety guidance;
- treating review count as quality or fit proof;
- copying VaporDNA's taxonomy, campaigns, prices, product mix, copy, imagery, layout, or trade dress.

## 5. What vape/nicotine-specific requirements are beginning to emerge?

- normalized product role, platform, brand/model, technical specification, nicotine format, and promotion fields;
- separate schemas for disposables, refillable devices/kits, e-liquid, pouches, tanks, replacements, and accessories;
- bidirectional compatibility and replacement graphs;
- included/required/optional/replacement component states;
- device-first and part-first discovery;
- compatibility-aware actions, recommendations, search, cart, and sold-out alternatives;
- explicit flavor, nicotine strength/format, capacity, and nicotine-free state;
- technical claim provenance and type-aware comparison;
- lifecycle distinction among device, consumable, replacement, accessory, and disposable;
- progressive setup, charging, maintenance, and replacement education;
- age, destination eligibility, nicotine warning, technical safety, inventory, and compatibility as separate responsibilities;
- contextual replenishment only after product relationships are reliable.

These remain candidates until deeper product and compatibility evidence and Blowin' Smoke's real catalog, operations, and compliance decisions validate them.

## 6. What is the single highest-value next research target?

**VaporDNA's compatibility / replacement experience, tested as one device-to-component and component-to-device journey.**

It would show whether VaporDNA converts model names and technical products into an actual error-prevention system: what fits, what is included, what is required, what must be replaced, which alternatives remain valid, and how those relationships change the action.
