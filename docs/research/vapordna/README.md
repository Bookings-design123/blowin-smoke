# VaporDNA Research

## Purpose

VaporDNA is being studied as a vape, nicotine, device, and consumables commerce reference for the Blowin' Smoke project.

VaporDNA is NOT being used as the primary visual-design or UI inspiration.

Glossier research provides much of the broader interface discipline, interaction philosophy, progressive disclosure, product-state architecture, and premium digital-experience principles being considered for Blowin' Smoke.

Bay Smokes research provides THCA-specific domain intelligence.

VaporDNA has a different assignment:

Understand how an established vape retailer organizes, explains, merchandises, and sells a technically complex ecosystem of devices, consumables, replacement components, flavors, nicotine products, and accessories.

The objective is to extract domain-specific commerce intelligence that can inform the Vape & Nicotine division of Blowin' Smoke.

## Research Objectives

### Product Taxonomy

Study how VaporDNA distinguishes:

- disposable vapes
- refillable devices
- pod systems
- vape kits
- tanks
- rebuildables / DIY
- coils
- replacement pods
- cartridges
- vape juice / e-liquid
- nicotine pouches
- accessories
- replacement components
- brands

Separate:

PRODUCT TYPE

DEVICE PLATFORM

CONSUMABLE

REPLACEMENT COMPONENT

ACCESSORY

BRAND

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION

PROMOTIONAL COLLECTION

Do not collapse these dimensions into one taxonomy.

### Compatibility

Compatibility is a critical research objective.

Determine how the commerce system helps a customer understand:

- which pods fit which devices
- which coils fit which tanks/devices
- which replacement components belong to which platform
- which consumables require particular hardware
- whether products are cross-compatible
- whether a purchase requires another component
- whether an accessory is universal or device-specific

Identify where incompatible-purchase risk exists and how it is prevented or reduced.

## Blowin' Smoke Application

The purpose of this research is NOT to make Blowin' Smoke look like VaporDNA.

The purpose is to understand the vape customer's decision environment.

The Vape & Nicotine division must eventually combine:

- Blowin' Smoke's Brand Philosophy
- Glossier-derived interface discipline
- VaporDNA-derived vape-domain intelligence
- accurate technical product data
- compatibility-aware commerce
- transparent product relationships
- original Blowin' Smoke curation

The customer should not need expert-level vape knowledge to avoid purchasing the wrong product.

---

## What We Are NOT Copying

Do not reproduce VaporDNA's:

- branding
- logo
- copywriting
- photography
- color palette
- typography
- page layouts
- trade dress
- promotional campaigns
- exact navigation
- exact product cards
- exact merchandising language

Do not assume an industry practice is good merely because VaporDNA uses it.

Research should distinguish between:

WHAT EXISTS

WHY IT EXISTS

WHETHER IT WORKS

WHETHER BLOWIN' SMOKE SHOULD ADAPT, IMPROVE, OR REJECT IT

---

## Governing Research Question

Every major conclusion should answer:

What does a vape or nicotine customer need to know, understand, compare, verify, or match in order to purchase the correct product—and how can Blowin' Smoke make that decision easier and harder to get wrong?

---

## Deliverable

The final VaporDNA research should define candidate domain requirements for the Blowin' Smoke Vape & Nicotine division.

Special emphasis must be placed on:

1. product taxonomy;
2. technical specifications;
3. device-consumable compatibility;
4. replacement-component relationships;
5. product discovery;
6. comparison;
7. nicotine/flavor configuration;
8. availability and product states;
9. replenishment;
10. prevention of incompatible or mistaken purchases.

These requirements must eventually integrate with the existing Blowin' Smoke system rather than create a disconnected second website inside the brand.
