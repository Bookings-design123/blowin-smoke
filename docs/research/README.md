# Blowin' Smoke Research

This directory contains all external research used to inform the design and architecture of Blowin' Smoke.

Research is organized by inspiration source.

The purpose of research is not to copy websites.

The purpose is to identify reusable principles, components, interaction patterns, merchandising strategies, and design systems that can be adapted into an original Blowin' Smoke experience.

Every research source should answer a specific question.

## Research Sources

- Glossier — UI, design language, spacing, typography, component system.
- Bay Smokes — THCA commerce architecture.
- VaporDNA — Vape and nicotine commerce architecture.
- Smoke Cartel — Glass and accessories commerce architecture.

Every conclusion should be evidence-based and traceable to the research.
