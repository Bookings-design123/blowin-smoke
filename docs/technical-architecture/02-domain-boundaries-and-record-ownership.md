# Domain Boundaries and Record Ownership

## Ownership rules

Each domain below is a logical system of record even while its storage/provider is **NOT SELECTED**. Stable identifiers are Blowin' Smoke canonical identifiers; external IDs are aliases. Writes require authority, provenance, optimistic version control and audit. Current, stale, unknown, invalidated and service-error states remain explicit. Public, private, sensitive and restricted-document classifications drive access and retention.

## Cross-domain invariant

No domain may infer another domain's positive state. In particular: price is not stock; stock is not eligibility; proof is not approval; related is not compatible; compatibility is not physical fit; a provider response is not canonical until its owning domain validates and records it; a projection is never its own system of record.
