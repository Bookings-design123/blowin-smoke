## Part I — System Constitution

Blowin' Smoke believes commerce should turn independent judgment and product knowledge into customer confidence. The interface must help a person understand what a product is, whether it applies to them, whether it works with what they own, what evidence supports it, what else it requires, and what will happen after purchase.

### Governing translations

| BRAND PRINCIPLE | DESIGN CONSEQUENCE | COMMERCE CONSEQUENCE | TECHNICAL CONSEQUENCE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authenticity must be earned | Use direct language, real provenance, and visible uncertainty; avoid borrowed cultural signals | Recommend only what has a defensible reason to be carried or paired | Store claim type, source, status, and owner; do not hard-code hype as fact |
| Beautiful design is respect | Make hierarchy calm, purposeful, accessible, and easy to scan | Put decision-critical truth before persuasion | Treat accessibility, resilience, and performance as architecture |
| Knowledge is a product | Explain terminology when it becomes relevant | Use education to prevent wrong purchases, not merely drive traffic | Connect guidance to products, options, relationships, and post-purchase context |
| Independence over imitation | Express a distinct house point of view through curation and standards | Explain why a product earned attention | Keep competitor research outside catalog authority and production data |
| Customer respect over manipulation | Preserve agency, dismissibility, honest states, and clear consequences | No fake urgency, hidden terms, deceptive defaults, or promotion-first decisions | Require explicit consent, recoverable state, and transparent errors |
| Substance over appearance | Media and interaction must answer a question | Proof, compatibility, contents, and eligibility outrank lifestyle claims | Essential truth must survive missing media, JavaScript failure, and network delay |
| Performance over theater | Motion, surfaces, and copy have defined jobs | Actions match actual readiness; failures explain recovery | Minimize layout shift, preserve state integrity, and instrument decision failures |
| Culture through participation | Represent real operators, customers, makers, and convictions with consent | Curation reflects firsthand judgment rather than generic trend ranking | Provenance supports authorship, permissions, dates, and accountable ownership |

### Operational meanings

- **Customer respect** means no required fact is hidden to simplify conversion; no state is made to look resolved when it is not; support retains context; consent is never assumed.
- **Authenticity** means published facts have owners, recommendations have reasons, mistakes can be corrected visibly, and the brand admits unknowns.
- **Independent judgment** appears as selective assortment, explicit rationale, original education, and willingness to reject popular but unsuitable products.
- **Knowledge is a product** means the system improves the customer's ability to choose, use, maintain, verify, and replace—not just their ability to click Add.
- **Premium** means decision quality, controlled hierarchy, responsive feedback, dependable performance, accessible operation, and trustworthy follow-through. It does not mean beauty-brand minimalism or expensive-looking decoration.
- **Restraint** means one dominant job per surface, controlled emphasis, progressive disclosure, limited badges, and motion with purpose. It does not mean withholding useful information.

### Refusals

The system refuses copied trade dress, three disconnected stores, culture-as-costume, promotion ahead of truth, arbitrary recommendations, unidentified claims, manufactured certainty, accessibility as cleanup, and conversion tactics that increase preventable purchase errors.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** Every major design or commerce proposal must pass both Article I's Decision Test and a decision-quality test: *Does this help the customer reach the correct, supportable action without obscuring risk or uncertainty?*

Blowin' Smoke is one brand, one account, one catalog language, one cart, and one trust system with three specialized divisions:

1. **THCA**
2. **Vape & Nicotine**
3. **Glass & Accessories**

| Layer | SHARED ACROSS ALL DIVISIONS | CHANGES BY DIVISION | CHANGES BY PRODUCT TYPE |

| Navigation | One hierarchy, consistent interaction and accessibility | Category vocabulary and education routes | Deep routes such as strain, device platform, or fitted component |
| Search | One query system and result grammar | Division-aware interpretation and ranking | Searchable attributes and compatibility relationships |
| Product card | Stable media, identity, facts, price, state, action slots | Controlled proof/fit/fact slots | Two or three role-critical facts and truthful CTA |
| PDP | One orientation-to-support hierarchy | Division-specific proof and eligibility modules | Option schema, specifications, compatibility, contents, and care |
| Price | One current/compare/from/bundle grammar | Division-specific purchase methods only when real | Variant- or quantity-dependent computation |
| Availability | One vocabulary and action precedence | Eligibility may limit availability interpretation | Variant, batch, component, or made-to-order state |
| Recommendations | Every item has a reason and state validation | Domain-specific relationship types | Compatibility, replenishment, care, or contextual use |
| Education | Point-of-decision progressive disclosure | Domain terminology and responsible guidance | Setup, measurement, storage, proof, or maintenance |

### Shared system contract

- **Global shell:** persistent orientation without crowding product content.
- **Navigation:** durable division taxonomy separated from temporary campaigns.

- **Account:** saved context only with consent; order, product, proof, and support continuity.
- **Cart:** a decision checkpoint, not a passive SKU list.
- **Qualification:** age and destination logic are shared capabilities, populated by current rules later.
- **Trust:** fact, proof, compatibility, provenance, policy, and testimony remain distinct.
- **Accessibility:** identical obligations in every division and state.

**RESEARCH-DERIVED PRINCIPLE:** Stable component grammar, progressive disclosure, synchronized state, and action matched to readiness transfer from the Glossier research.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** Division distinction is carried by information and curated expression—not separate shells, arbitrary colors, or incompatible interaction patterns.

## Part III — Shared Product Ontology

| Object | Conceptual meaning |
|---|---|
| PRODUCT | The durable customer-facing product concept that groups related sellable configurations |
| SELLABLE VARIANT | The exact purchasable configuration with its own option values, price, stock, eligibility, proof, media, and identifiers |
| PRODUCT TYPE | What the object is: flower, disposable, coil, bong, cleaner, and so on |
| PRODUCT ROLE | How it participates in use: complete product, consumable, replacement, fitted component, accessory, care product, or service-enabling object |
| DIVISION | THCA, Vape & Nicotine, or Glass & Accessories; one product has a primary division even when relationships cross divisions |
| BRAND | The customer-facing commercial identity attached to a product |
| MAKER / MANUFACTURER / ARTIST | The accountable creator or producer; these roles must not be collapsed when they differ |
| COLLECTION | A durable or editorial grouping; not a substitute for product type or attributes |
| PROMOTIONAL STATE | A bounded merchandising label such as New, Sale, Featured, or Curated, with owner and validity period |
| PRICE STATE | Current, compare-at, from, option-dependent, bundle, or approved recurring price and its conditions |
| AVAILABILITY STATE | Whether the exact sellable configuration can be supplied now, later, or not at all |
| ELIGIBILITY STATE | Whether the customer and destination may proceed under rules supplied by qualified owners |
| PROOF STATE | Whether decision-relevant evidence exists, applies to the selected variant or batch, is current, and is accessible |
| COMPATIBILITY STATE | Verified fit relationship: Compatible, Incompatible, Conditionally Compatible, Universal, Unknown/Unverified, or Not Applicable |
| MEDIA | Authored evidence-bearing assets with role, subject, variant scope, alternative, and provenance |
| EDUCATION | Decision support connected to a term, task, product, relationship, or post-purchase need |
| RELATIONSHIP | A typed, directional or bidirectional connection among products, variants, components, proof, guides, or successors |
| CLAIM | A statement classified by kind, scope, evidence, owner, status, and expiration where relevant |
| SOURCE / PROVENANCE | The origin and authority record for any fact, relationship, claim, or asset |

### Ontology rules

1. Product identity, type, role, maker, attributes, promotion, and collection remain separate dimensions.
2. Variant-dependent facts live with the variant and never leak from another option.
3. Relationships are typed; “related” alone is not sufficient.
4. Unknown, not applicable, and not supplied are different values.
5. A visual label never substitutes for semantic state.
6. Product titles identify; normalized fields explain and enable comparison.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** This ontology is the shared language all later page, data, search, recommendation, cart, support, and analytics specifications must use.

## Part IV — State Architecture

### State precedence

The universal evaluation order is:

1. **Age qualification**
2. **Destination eligibility**
3. **Product-specific eligibility**
4. **Product and selected-variant availability**
5. **Variant completeness**
6. **Compatibility**
7. **Required components**
8. **Proof requirements**
9. **Price resolution**
10. **Purchase-method completeness**
11. **CTA readiness**

Higher-risk unresolved states cannot be overridden by lower-level convenience or promotion.

### Universal action states

| Composite state | Meaning | Primary behavior |
|---|---|---|
| READY TO PURCHASE | All required selections and high-risk checks resolve; price and availability are valid | Enable the truthful purchase action |
| SELECTION REQUIRED | One or more required option dimensions are unresolved | Identify the missing choice; move focus to it |
| COMPATIBILITY CHECK REQUIRED | Fit is material and no sufficient context or verified relationship exists | Request owned-product/measurement context or route to help |
| RESTRICTED | Age, destination, or product-specific eligibility fails | Block purchase; explain the category of rule and available non-legal support |
| UNAVAILABLE | The selected sellable state cannot be supplied | Disable purchase; offer valid recovery routes |
| NOTIFY ELIGIBLE | Unavailable state permits permission-based restock notice | Offer scoped notification without implying timing |
| UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED | Material truth cannot be established | Disclose uncertainty; block, warn, or escalate according to risk |
| ADDITIONAL COMPONENT REQUIRED | Current product cannot perform its intended role without another item | Name the requirement and prevent an incomplete “ready” representation |

### Composition rules

- One product can hold several states simultaneously; the interface shows the highest-priority blocker and preserves lower-level context.
- **Restricted** outranks stock, discount, and purchase method.
- **Unavailable** does not erase compatibility or proof information needed for alternatives.
- **Selection Required** resolves before price or CTA can claim finality.
- **Incompatible** blocks a claimed compatible setup or bundle.
- **Unknown/Unverified** never becomes Compatible, Lab Tested, Eligible, Included, or Available by omission.
- An informational warning may allow purchase only when the unresolved condition does not create a known legal, safety, or unusable-product risk and the customer can understand the consequence.
- State changes update media, facts, proof, price, availability, URL where appropriate, and accessible announcements atomically.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** CTA text is an output of the composed state model, not an independent merchandising choice.

## Part V — Shared Product Card System

### Universal card grammar

Every product card uses the following semantic order:

1. media;
2. division label when context is mixed;
3. brand, maker, or artist when it changes the decision;
4. concise product identity;
5. product role/type;
6. two or three decision-critical facts;
7. proof or compatibility status when material;
8. price state;
9. one controlled promotional state, if warranted;
10. availability;
11. truthful next action.

The shell remains stable. Division extensions populate controlled fact slots:

- **THCA:** format, quantity/strain context, and proof or eligibility signal.
- **Vape & Nicotine:** role/platform, nicotine or capacity fact, and compatibility signal.
- **Glass & Accessories:** type/material or scale fact, connection fact, and fit signal.

### Card rules

- The title never carries the full data model.
- A card exposes only options that can be understood and resolved safely in its compact context.
- Complex, regulated, or fit-dependent configuration routes to the PDP or a focused resolver.
- Hover may reveal useful secondary media, but focus and touch receive equivalent access.
- Price, stock, eligibility, proof, and fit states remain readable without relying on color.
- A promotional badge cannot displace a blocker or decision-critical fact.

### Excluded from cards

- long descriptions or education dumps;
- complete specification tables;
- unlabeled technical abbreviations;
- more than one primary promotion badge;
- unscoped claims or testimonial fragments;
- critical facts available only in imagery, alt text, filenames, hover, or URLs;
- arbitrary recommendation rationales;
- direct Add when selection, eligibility, compatibility, contents, or price is unresolved.

**RESEARCH-DERIVED PRINCIPLE:** A stable card can support different commercial objects when its action changes with readiness.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** The card is a qualification surface, not a miniature PDP or promotional billboard.

## Part VI — Shared PDP System

### Universal hierarchy

1. orientation and division context;
2. product identity and maker/brand;
3. essential decision facts;
4. configuration and options;
5. price;
6. availability;
7. eligibility;
8. compatibility and/or proof;
9. purchase action;
10. decision-oriented media;
11. deeper product truth and specifications;
12. education, setup, care, or measurement help;
13. reviews and social proof;
14. contextual recommendations;
15. policy and support.

The visual arrangement may interleave media and purchase content, but the semantic and state dependencies remain intact.

### Disclosure placement

| Placement | Required content |
|---|---|
| ABOVE THE PRIMARY ACTION | Identity; selected variant; essential facts; required options; resolved price; availability; material age/destination/product eligibility; material proof or fit state; included/required-component warning |
| NEAR THE PRIMARY ACTION | Concise fulfillment context; return limitation if decision-critical; payment or replenishment terms; support route; state-specific explanation and recovery |
| BELOW THE PRIMARY ACTION | Full specifications; contents; maker/provenance; product proof; media sequence; care/setup; reviews; reasoned recommendations |
| DEEP DISCLOSURE | Detailed methods, complete documents, glossary, legal/policy text, long-form guides, full review controls, and archival proof history |

### PDP behavior

- Required option dimensions are explicit, independently named, and resolved atomically.
- Selected state synchronizes URL where appropriate, media, facts, proof, price, stock, eligibility, compatibility, and CTA.
- The first media sequence answers recognition, differentiation, scale, and included-content questions before editorial persuasion.
- Accordions reduce density but never conceal a purchase blocker.
- Recommendations appear only after the primary decision is understandable.
- Reviews remain subordinate to verified facts.

**RESEARCH-DERIVED PRINCIPLE:** Progressive disclosure, state synchronization, proof layering, and contextual cross-selling form the shared PDP discipline.

## Part VII — THCA Division Extension

### THCA commerce contract

The THCA model separates product family, product type, format, strain, quantity, sellable variant, cannabinoid/composition facts, potency semantics, batch, proof documents, eligibility, fulfillment, claims, and media.

### THCA card extension

Use the shared card plus:

- product type/format;
- selected or representative quantity with clear price basis;
- strain when it identifies the sellable choice;
- one decision-relevant composition/potency fact only when properly scoped;
- COA/proof status and eligibility signal when material.

Do not show effect promises, unscoped potency, testimonial claims, or a generic “lab tested” badge without product/batch linkage.

### THCA PDP extension

Above or near action, resolve:

- format and product identity;
- strain and quantity as independent option dimensions;
- selected-variant price and unit basis where useful;
- current availability;
- age, destination, and product-specific eligibility state;
- cannabinoid/composition and potency semantics with scope;
- COA/proof state linked to the selected product or batch;
- fulfillment limitation that could change the purchase decision.

Below action, provide full proof, factual product description, storage/handling, responsible education, claim classification, reviews with option context, and reasoned complements or replenishment.

### THCA uncertainty states

- **COA UNKNOWN:** no applicable document relationship is known.
- **COA NOT SUPPLIED:** the expected source has not supplied a document.
- **COA STALE:** the document exists but is outside its accepted currency rule.
- **BATCH NOT MATCHED:** proof exists but cannot be tied to selected inventory.
- **POTENCY UNVERIFIED:** a value is published without accepted authority.
- **ELIGIBILITY UNKNOWN:** current rule coverage is missing; never default to eligible.
- **CLAIM CONFLICTING:** product, document, and catalog statements disagree.

### Governance

- Laboratory results, marketing descriptions, policy statements, and customer testimony never silently change categories.
- Sensory or preference language is labeled as such and cannot become a health, effect, or outcome guarantee.
- Reviews may describe experience, packaging, aroma, flavor preference, and service; they cannot establish composition, potency, legality, or effects.

**DOMAIN REQUIREMENT:** Bay Smokes research established the need for normalized THCA options, product-linked proof, eligibility, discreet fulfillment, and claim governance.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** The complete THCA state architecture and proof-to-variant relationship are original and must be populated from authoritative catalog, laboratory, and compliance sources.

## Part VIII — Vape & Nicotine Division Extension

### Specialized product roles

- **Disposable:** integrated device and consumable with a finite lifecycle.
- **Refillable device:** durable platform requiring compatible consumables and replacements.
- **Kit:** a sold configuration with an explicit contents and dependency contract.
- **Consumable:** depleted through normal use.
- **E-liquid:** consumable with flavor, nicotine, composition, volume, and hardware-suitability attributes.
- **Nicotine pouch:** consumable with strength, count, format, and flavor attributes.
- **Pod:** reservoir/interface component tied to one or more platforms.
- **Coil:** replacement heating component with fit and operating constraints.
- **Cartridge:** replaceable reservoir or atomizing component with a defined platform relationship.
- **Tank:** reservoir/atomizer assembly with connection, coil, capacity, and power relationships.
- **Replacement:** restores a defined device or component function.
- **Accessory:** optional or required item whose universal or platform-specific scope must be explicit.

### Conceptual technical schemas

| Product role | Decision-critical schema candidates |
|---|---|
| Disposable | model, nicotine format/strength, flavor, liquid capacity, battery/charging, marketed puff claim with provenance, coil technology, lifecycle |
| Refillable device/kit | platform/model, battery, charging, power range, included components, required pod/tank/coil, supported replacements |
| E-liquid | volume, nicotine format, nicotine strength, flavor, composition where governed, suitable hardware class |
| Nicotine pouch | strength basis, count, format, flavor, ingredients/composition where required |
| Pod/cartridge | platform fit, capacity, coil inclusion, refillability, resistance/options |
| Coil | supported platforms/tanks, resistance, recommended power range, material/technology, replacement unit/count |
| Tank | connection, capacity, supported coils, power range, included glass/coil/components |
| Accessory/replacement | role, supported platform/model, required versus optional status, lifecycle |

All claims identify units, variant scope, source, and verification status. Manufacturer puff counts remain manufacturer claims unless independently measured under a documented method.

### Vape & Nicotine card extension

Use the shared card plus role, platform/model, and two critical fields such as nicotine strength, capacity, resistance, or compatibility. Separate brand/model from specifications. Show fit status when the product is a component or accessory.

### Vape & Nicotine PDP extension

Resolve above or near action: product role, platform/model, variant, nicotine configuration, included and required components, compatibility state, critical power/resistance constraints, selected price, stock, eligibility, and CTA. Place complete specifications, setup, charging, replacement lifecycle, technical claim provenance, and care below action.

**DOMAIN REQUIREMENT:** Device, consumable, replacement, accessory, and disposable purchases have different decision and lifecycle requirements.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** Product-role schemas and state behavior are original; no unverified VaporDNA mechanism or relationship is precedent.

## Part IX — Original Electronic Compatibility Architecture

This entire architecture is an **ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION**.

### Relationship graph

```text
DEVICE / PLATFORM
        ↕
POD / TANK / CARTRIDGE
        ↕
COIL / REPLACEMENT COMPONENT
        ↕
COMPATIBLE CONSUMABLE
        ↕
ACCESSORY
```

Each relationship records source object, target object, relationship type, direction, variant scope, conditions, required intermediary, evidence source, verification status, verifier, and date.

### Fit states

- **COMPATIBLE:** verified for the exact relationship and relevant variants.
- **INCOMPATIBLE:** a known conflict prevents intended use.
- **CONDITIONALLY COMPATIBLE:** works only under stated hardware, power, adapter, version, or configuration conditions.
- **UNIVERSAL:** verified across a clearly bounded class; never an unqualified synonym for “probably fits.”
- **UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED:** evidence is insufficient.
- **NOT APPLICABLE:** fit does not govern this relationship.

### Customer journeys

- **Device-first:** select or recall a device → view verified pods/tanks/cartridges → compatible coils/consumables/accessories → current stock and successors.
- **Component-first:** search model/part/resistance → see supported devices/platforms → verify selected variant and operating conditions.
- **Compatibility-aware search:** interpret model aliases and part numbers; show why a result fits or why status is unknown.
- **Recommendations and bundles:** validate every fit-dependent member; state whether the relationship is required, replacement, compatible, care, or optional.
- **Cart checks:** block verified incompatibility; identify missing required components; warn and route unknown fit according to risk.
- **Sold-out alternatives:** distinguish exact replacement, compatible alternative, successor, and merely similar product.
- **Replacement/reorder:** preserve owned platform, prior variant, replacement interval context, and current compatibility verification.
- **Unknown-fit escalation:** send product, model, selected options, candidate part, and known evidence to support without forcing the customer to restart.

Unknown fit never silently becomes compatible. Similar names, connector appearance, review statements, or co-purchase history are not fit evidence.

This entire architecture is an **ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION**.

### Fit record

A relationship may require nominal size, gender/type on both sides, angle, total/effective length, orientation, clearance envelope, adapter requirement, load/weight consideration, variant, physical tolerance, handmade variation, material applicability, evidence source, test method, verifier, and verification date.

### Customer journeys

- **Owned-piece-first:** identify an owned catalog item or enter verified measurements; retrieve compatible parts and care.
- **Component-first:** select a bowl, banger, downstem, catcher, or adapter; show supported pieces/specifications and conditions.
- **Measurement-first help:** teach how to identify size, gender/type, angle, effective length, and clearance; never infer from a photograph.
- **Unknown-fit escalation:** preserve owned-piece identity, measurements, images supplied with consent, candidate part, and unresolved field.
- **Cart validation:** block verified incompatibility; explain conditional fit and adapters; warn/escalate unknown fit.
- **Compatible alternatives:** match the required relationship and physical constraints, not just product type or nominal size.
- **Care applicability:** connect cleaners and methods to verified materials, finishes, removable parts, and prohibited uses.

Handmade variation and manufacturing tolerance must be represented as conditions or ranges, not hidden behind nominal values.

## Part XII — Media System

### Universal principles

Media must answer a customer question, correspond to the selected variant, identify its role, and have an accessible alternative. It cannot be the sole carrier of critical specification, contents, proof, compatibility, or eligibility information.

| Media role | Customer question |
|---|---|
| IDENTITY VIEW | What is the exact product? |
| SCALE VIEW | How large, heavy, or spatially demanding is it? |
| CONNECTION VIEW | Where and how does it attach? |
| CONTENTS VIEW | What is actually included? |
| ASSEMBLY VIEW | How do the parts orient and connect? |
| MATERIAL / CRAFT VIEW | What construction, finish, texture, or workmanship matters? |
| USE-CONTEXT VIEW | How is it positioned, handled, or used responsibly? |
| VARIANT VIEW | What does the exact sellable option look like? |

### Division extensions

- **THCA:** factual product and packaging identity, format, quantity, texture where useful, and meaningful strain/variant differentiation. Media must not imply potency, effects, or proof.
- **Vape & Nicotine:** device identity, controls, ports, included components, pod/coil relationship, scale, configuration, and exact color/variant.
- **Glass & Accessories:** standardized scale, joints, angles, contents, assembly, material/craft, footprint, and maker detail.

Video or motion media must provide controls, captions or equivalent explanation where needed, static alternatives, and reduced-motion equivalence. Responsive crops are authored by role rather than indiscriminately scaled.

## Part XIII — Search & Discovery

### One search language

Search indexes normalized identity, brand, maker, product, product type, role, strain, format, model, platform, part number, joint size, gender/type, angle, material, flavor, nicotine strength, compatibility, common synonyms, abbreviations, and misspellings.

It distinguishes a term's meaning rather than matching marketing strings blindly. Ambiguous terms prompt scoped refinement, such as division, role, owned product, or measurement.

### Result contract

Every result communicates division, product role, concise identity, critical facts, price, availability, eligibility when known and material, proof/compatibility status, and truthful action. Explanations such as “fits your saved device” require verified relationship and consented context.

### Discovery modes

- **KNOWN ITEM:** exact product, model, part, strain, or maker.
- **CATEGORY:** durable taxonomy and normalized filters.
- **PROBLEM / NEED:** guided route such as cleaning, replacement, beginner setup, or discreet fulfillment.
- **COMPATIBILITY:** start from owned product or candidate component.
- **REPLACEMENT:** exact, compatible, or successor relationship.
- **EDUCATION:** terminology and decision guidance with product routes secondary.
- **CURATION:** an edited selection with an explicit rationale.

Zero results preserve interpreted constraints, explain which constraint failed, suggest safe relaxation, and route unresolved fit or eligibility questions to support. Search never converts unknown fit or missing proof into a positive result claim.

## Part XIV — Recommendation & Bundle Governance

| Type | Required reason |
|---|---|
| COMPLEMENTARY | Adds a distinct, non-required use value |
| COMPATIBLE | Verified to fit the selected product/variant and conditions |
| REPLACEMENT | Restores the same function for a supported product |
| SUCCESSOR | Documented replacement for a discontinued product, with differences disclosed |
| CARE | Applicable to the verified material, device, or maintenance need |
| CURATED | Selected through a stated Blowin' Smoke standard or point of view |
| BUNDLE | A coherent set whose contents, dependencies, price, and fit are explicit |
| REPLENISHMENT | Contextually timed repeat of a consumable or replacement, not generic pressure |

Recommendations may appear after the primary decision is understandable, within a guided setup, during valid sold-out recovery, or post-purchase when context supports them. They may not appear as substitutes for unresolved options, eligibility, proof, or fit; they may not use “customers also bought” as compatibility evidence.

Every module states or makes available: relationship type, reason, scope, validation status, and any condition. Empty or unsafe modules disappear cleanly rather than filling with arbitrary inventory.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** “You may also like” is not a sufficient relationship.

## Part XVI — Trust, Proof & Claim Governance

### Claim classes

| Class | Meaning | Permitted authority examples |
|---|---|---|
| PRODUCT FACT | Stable identity or catalog fact | Blowin' Smoke verified record; authoritative maker/manufacturer documentation |
| MEASURED FACT | Value produced by a documented measurement | Blowin' Smoke validation or qualified laboratory/maker method |
| MANUFACTURER CLAIM | Statement supplied by the producer but not independently verified | Manufacturer documentation, clearly attributed |
| LABORATORY RESULT | Result tied to method, sample, batch, date, and laboratory | Applicable COA/laboratory source |
| LEGAL / POLICY STATEMENT | Rule or policy supplied by its qualified owner | Legal/compliance or operations owner |
| OPERATIONAL PROMISE | Shipping, support, return, warranty, or service commitment | Accountable Blowin' Smoke operations owner |
| MARKETING OPINION | Subjective positioning or curation statement | Named content/merchandising owner; never presented as fact |
| CUSTOMER TESTIMONY | A customer's contextual experience | Verified/moderated review record |
| COMPATIBILITY EVIDENCE | Evidence that two exact products/specifications work together | Authoritative documentation or documented Blowin' Smoke validation |
| MAKER / PROVENANCE CLAIM | Authorship, origin, material, method, or studio history | Maker/artist documentation and verified commercial record |

### Evidence status

- **VERIFIED:** accepted authority, scope, and currency requirements are met.
- **UNVERIFIED:** a statement exists but has not met the verification rule.
- **UNKNOWN:** no reliable determination is available.
- **STALE:** evidence exists but is outside its currency requirement.
- **NOT APPLICABLE:** the evidence class does not govern the object.

No claim silently changes category. A review cannot become a product fact; a manufacturer claim cannot become a measured fact; a marketing phrase cannot become eligibility guidance; a COA cannot automatically apply to another batch.

Every published claim has source type, owner, reference, scope, status, verification date, last update, and expiration where material. Corrections must propagate to every surface consuming the claim.

## Part XVII — Eligibility, Safety & Responsible Commerce

The system separates:

| Concern | Question answered |
|---|---|
| AGE QUALIFICATION | Has the customer met the current minimum-age requirement supplied by the policy owner? |
| DESTINATION ELIGIBILITY | May this exact product be supplied to the entered destination under current rules? |
| PRODUCT-SPECIFIC ELIGIBILITY | Does another rule apply to this product, format, quantity, or purchase method? |
| NICOTINE WARNING | What responsible-use information is required for nicotine products? |
| TECHNICAL SAFETY | What operating, charging, power, resistance, heating, or handling constraints apply? |
| PRODUCT SAFETY | What storage, handling, material, breakage, or care constraints apply? |
| COMPATIBILITY | Will the selected products work together as intended? |
| INVENTORY | Can the selected sellable variant be supplied? |

One positive state never implies another. In stock does not mean eligible, compatible, safe for a configuration, or supported by proof.

Qualified legal/compliance owners will later supply jurisdiction, product, effective-date, and policy rules. The design must support versioned rules, clear explanations, privacy-conscious qualification, failure recovery, and accessible updates without writing legal conclusions itself.

Restricted purchases are blocked. Unknown eligibility does not default to allowed. The interface explains the category of restriction without improvising legal advice.

## Part XX — Motion & Interaction Philosophy

**RESEARCH-DERIVED PRINCIPLE:** Motion is valuable when it communicates topology, sequence, state, or feedback; the exact Glossier expression is excluded.

Blowin' Smoke uses motion for:

- **state change:** confirm option, cart, proof, availability, or compatibility updates;
- **orientation:** explain entry/exit of drawers, dialogs, and navigation layers;
- **hierarchy:** disclose supporting information without rearranging the decision unexpectedly;
- **feedback:** acknowledge action, loading, success, and failure promptly;
- **progressive disclosure:** reveal detail while preserving context.

Reject decorative motion without a job, delayed actions, inaccessible hover-only information, excessive parallax, autoplay distraction, layout-shifting reveals, and animation that hides or postpones product truth.

Motion tokens will later describe semantic roles rather than competitor effects. Sound never starts without intent. Looping media exposes pause. Reduced-motion users receive equivalent content, state clarity, and completion—not a diminished experience.

## Part XXI — Accessibility

Accessibility is a prerequisite of the system architecture.

- Every function is operable by keyboard without timing traps.
- Focus order follows the decision hierarchy; visible focus is never removed.
- Drawers and true dialogs manage focus, prevent background interaction, support Escape where appropriate, and return focus meaningfully.
- Controls use native semantics where possible and accurate accessible names.

- Reduced-motion preferences receive equivalent outcomes.
- Compatibility matrices and specification groups use semantic relationships, not color-only diagrams.
- Loading states expose progress or busy status without stealing focus.

No division or third-party widget is exempt.

## Part XXII — Operational States

| State | Required interface response |
|---|---|
| LOADING | Preserve layout, name the pending object, expose accessible busy state, and prevent duplicate actions |

The interface fails honestly, locally, and recoverably. It never replaces missing truth with confidence theater.

## Part XXIII — Data Provenance & Governance

### Conceptual authority hierarchy

1. **Blowin' Smoke verified data** — accepted internal truth produced or validated under a governed process.
2. **Qualified legal/compliance owner** — authority for eligibility, warning, and policy rules within its scope.
3. **Laboratory/COA data** — authority for the exact tested sample/batch, method, and reported result.
4. **Manufacturer/maker documentation** — authority for identity, intended specifications, contents, and supported relationships unless stronger validation supersedes it.
5. **Blowin' Smoke measurement/validation and physical receiving inspection** — authority for documented observed dimensions, contents, condition, and tested product-pair relationships.
6. **Distributor/supplier data** — provisional operational/catalog source requiring field-level acceptance.
7. **Customer-supplied information** — context for support or owned-product matching; not catalog truth without verification.
8. **Third-party platform data** — operational input governed by contract, mapping, and validation.
9. **Customer review/testimony** — experiential evidence only.
10. **Marketing copy** — opinion or campaign expression, never a source for critical facts.
11. **Competitor research** — problem/design-principle evidence only; never implementation or catalog truth.

Authority is field- and scope-specific. A laboratory is not the authority for retail price; a manufacturer is not the authority for Blowin' Smoke stock; internal measurement does not create legal eligibility.

### Governance by data class

| Data | Governing source rule |
|---|---|
| Identity | Verified catalog plus manufacturer/maker identity record |
| Specification | Authoritative documentation or documented Blowin' Smoke validation |
| Compatibility | Explicit manufacturer relationship or verified product-pair/specification validation |
| Ingredients/composition | Authoritative product documentation and applicable laboratory evidence |
| Potency | Applicable laboratory result with product/batch scope |
| COA/proof | Original document, issuer, method/sample/batch relationship, date, and status |
| Dimensions/materials | Maker/manufacturer documentation confirmed where needed by receiving/measurement |
| Included contents | Verified SKU/package record and receiving inspection |
| Price | Blowin' Smoke commerce owner/system of record |
| Availability | Current inventory/fulfillment source of record |
| Eligibility | Current rules from qualified compliance/legal owner |
| Claims | Claim owner plus class-appropriate evidence |
| Media | Asset owner, subject/variant mapping, permissions, and capture date |
| Recommendations | Verified product relationships plus merchandising rationale |

### Provenance fields

Every material datum can carry source type, source owner, source reference, verification status, verified by, verification date, last updated, confidence/authority, applicable scope, and expiration/staleness rule.

**ORIGINAL BLOWIN' SMOKE DECISION:** Derived displays may simplify authoritative data, but they must retain traceability to the governing record.

## Part XXX — Cart System

Cart is the final pre-checkout decision checkpoint. Each line item communicates exact product and variant, quantity, current price, availability, eligibility, proof issue when material, compatibility, missing required components, bundle relationship, and fulfillment context.

### Intervention modes

| Mode | Use |
|---|---|
| BLOCK | Verified restriction, verified incompatibility in a claimed setup, unavailable state, invalid price, or unresolved mandatory selection |
| WARN | Material uncertainty or changed state that can be understood without implying safety/legality |
| ALLOW WITH INFORMATION | Non-blocking condition, optional component, or contextual fulfillment note |
| REQUEST CUSTOMER CONFIRMATION | A clearly explained reversible choice whose risk is appropriate for customer acknowledgment |
| ROUTE TO SUPPORT | Identity, fit, proof, or eligibility cannot be resolved safely in the interface |

Verified incompatible components never silently proceed as a compatible set. Unknown fit triggers warning/help or blocking according to risk; it never receives a green fit state. Restricted products cannot proceed after eligibility failure. Required missing components are named with a reason. Bundle members retain their relationship and individual state.

Cart recalculates after any relevant product, inventory, price, proof, eligibility, or relationship update and explains changes without discarding valid customer choices. Checkout design is outside this specification.

## Part XXXIII — Security, Privacy & Customer Respect

Collect the minimum information necessary for the stated task and explain why it is requested.

- Age/location qualification collects only what the current rule and operating process require.
- Saved-device, owned-piece, or saved-product context is opt-in, editable, and removable.
- Personalization distinguishes service context from marketing consent.
- Marketing consent is granular, revocable, and never bundled with necessary transactions.
- Recommendation history and support context have defined retention and access controls.
- Customer-supplied measurements, images, product ownership, or purchase information are not reused beyond the disclosed purpose without consent.
- Sensitive or purchase-related information is not used to exploit vulnerability, infer identity, or create pressure.
- Third-party tools receive only necessary data and must not degrade consent, accessibility, or state integrity.

No interface may imply that consent is required when it is not, or hide refusal behind diminished service unrelated to the requested data.

## Part XL — Master System Summary

1. **What is Blowin' Smoke?** An independent commerce house for underdogs, builders, creators, and customers who value knowledge, authenticity, craftsmanship, and products chosen with conviction.
2. **What makes its digital commerce system different?** It treats decision quality as the product: product truth, proof, eligibility, compatibility, contents, and uncertainty shape every action.
3. **What is shared across all three divisions?** One shell, ontology, state model, card and PDP grammar, price and availability language, search, cart, trust, education, accessibility, motion, provenance, and support architecture.
4. **What intelligence is unique to THCA?** Product type/format, strain, quantity, composition, potency semantics, batch-linked proof/COA, current eligibility, fulfillment, and claim governance.
5. **What intelligence is unique to Vape & Nicotine?** Device/consumable/replacement roles, platform/model, nicotine configuration, technical specifications, electronic compatibility, included requirements, and lifecycle.
6. **What intelligence is unique to Glass & Accessories?** Material, dimensions, scale, joint size and gender/type, angle, length, clearance, physical fit, contents, maker provenance, care, and fragile-product operations.
7. **How does the system prevent bad purchases?** It composes eligibility, availability, selection, compatibility, required components, proof, price, and purchase method before producing the CTA; it blocks or escalates unresolved high-risk states.
8. **How does product knowledge become customer value?** Structured facts and point-of-decision education help customers discover, compare, verify, use, maintain, replace, and recover without needing insider vocabulary.
9. **How does the system remain original?** Article I governs identity; closure decisions prevent reference imitation; competitor evidence supplies principles and domain problems only; all integrated architecture is explicitly an original Blowin' Smoke decision.
10. **What must be true before implementation begins?** Foundational ontology, state, provenance, accessibility, compatibility, and source-of-truth contracts must be approved, and real catalog, operational, compliance, proof, measurement, media, policy, and support inputs must have accountable owners.

### Resolved synthesis conflicts

- **Restraint versus domain detail:** resolved through progressive disclosure. Critical facts remain near action; depth moves below without being hidden.
- **Quick Add versus decision risk:** resolved through state-derived actions. Quick Add exists only for fully resolved, low-risk variants.
- **Promotion versus independent voice:** resolved by using curation rationale and bounded labels after product truth.
- **Shared consistency versus division intelligence:** resolved through stable shells with governed extension slots, not separate design systems.
- **Customer autonomy versus protective checks:** resolved through risk-based block, warn, confirm, inform, and support modes; no false confidence or unexplained paternalism.
- **Reviews versus proof:** resolved by keeping testimony contextual and subordinate to authoritative facts.

No unresolved research-source conflict prevents this conceptual system. Implementation remains blocked where Part XXXIX identifies missing real-world inputs.
