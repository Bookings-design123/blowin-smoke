# Pressure & Proof — Iteration 03 Art-Direction Evaluation

**Evaluation status:** Comparative design research; user selection required

**Implementation authorization:** Not granted

## Evaluation basis

The three directions were judged against the Blowin’ Smoke Constitution, the governing commerce and page systems, the approved Pressure & Proof visual direction, and the Iteration 02 evaluation. Each uses the same five synthetic surfaces, fixture wording, state composition, and decision order.

The following committed evidence was opened and visually inspected:

- three full-page renders produced from a 1440 × 900 test viewport;
- three full-page renders produced from a 390 × 844 test viewport;
- one wide three-direction comparison board;
- one narrow three-direction comparison board;
- one grayscale comparison board;
- one approximately 25% thumbnail-scale comparison board.

The browser-reported document width equaled the requested canvas width for all six runs. A bounding-box audit found no critical horizontal overflow after the Constructed Signal media-frame correction. All visible links and buttons in the narrow audit met the package’s 44-pixel minimum control-height hypothesis. These checks do not establish complete accessibility conformance.

## Direction A — Poster Pressure

### Governing visual thesis

Blowin’ Smoke speaks with graphic pressure before asking for a transaction. Cropped display typography, abrupt field changes, asymmetry, and a small amount of upstream red punctuation create challenge and immediacy. As consequence rises, expression collapses into black witness rules, exact labels, full-width ledgers, and direct recovery.

### What makes it recognizably Blowin’ Smoke

- A challenge-led opening without artificial urgency.
- Heavy editorial scale paired with the Constitution’s direct language.
- Red used as brand punctuation only in the Home expression zone.
- Repeated witness bars, hard transitions, and explicit “truth before pressure” handoffs.
- Three divisions held in one house rather than styled as separate mini-brands.

### Recognition at PDP and Cart

The logo and red field disappear. Recognition relies on blunt typography, wide black rules, offset surface codes, a source/target break, and a consequence-first ledger. The Fitted PDP remains more ownable than the Universal PDP and Cart; the latter two need another revision to make Poster Pressure’s rule grammar unmistakable without increasing noise.

### Improvement over Iteration 02

It breaks the repeated eyebrow/heading/right-copy template most decisively. Home, Category, PDP, fit, and Cart no longer share one generic section silhouette. The Category separates the route strip from staggered qualified results, and Cart is a full-width consequence ledger rather than a sidebar layout.

### Weaknesses and gates

- The Home typography can overpower the house premise if real copy becomes longer.
- The direction could become generic streetwear if photography, copy, or motion reaches for easy rebellion.
- Category and Cart are clearer than Iteration 02 but lose some distinctive force after the major poster fields retreat.
- Extreme display typography requires browser, zoom, localization, and content-length stress tests.
- Authentic photography and voice are required to keep the direction culturally credible; CSS typography alone is not enough.

### Decision

**REVISE — Rank 3.** Keep it in consideration as the strongest campaign/upstream expression strategy. Do not advance it unchanged as the complete commerce language. The next revision would need a more proprietary low-pressure witness grammar before it can govern PDP and Cart.

## Direction B — Independent Archive

### Governing visual thesis

Blowin’ Smoke behaves like an independent operator who keeps accountable records: every object has a folio, every claim has a caption, and every relationship has a witness. Material texture, provenance, index numbers, and editorial pacing make commerce feel handled by real people rather than a distant system.

### What makes it recognizably Blowin’ Smoke

- Independent-house recordkeeping rather than corporate badge systems.
- Maker, source, batch, endpoint, and currentness captions as visible culture.
- An editorial paper rhythm that feels accumulated and handled, not cosmetically minimal.
- Direct language and human witness marks alongside exact data.
- A single house archive spanning all three divisions.

### Recognition at PDP and Cart

Folio numbering, registration lines, provenance captions, paired object sheets, and the numbered manifest continue after the logo disappears. P0 remains calm but is not anonymous. The brand accent was deliberately separated from incompatibility and cart semantics during inspection; the final state meaning comes from labels, symbols, and dedicated semantic aliases.

### Improvement over Iteration 02

It replaces generic cards with registers, sheets, paired folios, and a manifest. It creates editorial pacing between surfaces instead of repeating pale sections at one cadence. It also gives maker/provenance a visible role without treating reviews or imagery as product truth.

### Weaknesses and gates

- Fine captions and texture require contrast, zoom, low-vision, and print-like-density testing.
- Routine products could feel over-documented if every record receives the same archival weight.
- Synthetic paper effects risk fake heritage unless real maker stories, receiving records, and physical photography provide the material truth.
- The tone could drift toward museum distance or luxury catalog restraint; copy and imagery must retain warmth, edge, and present-tense culture.
- Real provenance fields, maker permissions, verified dates, and operational ownership are required before the archive can carry production meaning.

### Decision

**ADVANCE WITH REVISION — Rank 2.** This is a credible full-system candidate. It should remain under consideration, with a required density reduction study and authentic maker/material photography gate before selection.

## Direction C — Constructed Signal

### Governing visual thesis

Blowin’ Smoke’s advantage is made visible as a constructed relationship language. Routes, scope markers, endpoints, direct breaks, adapter bypasses, unknown segments, contributor chains, and terminal actions show how exact product truth moves through a decision.

### What makes it recognizably Blowin’ Smoke

- A proprietary expression of the project’s evidence and relationship model.
- One house signal spine connecting all three divisions.
- Unknowns shown as broken or dashed paths rather than soft affirmative cards.
- Direct plain language paired with scope and consequence notation.
- Decision confidence—not ornamental “premium” styling—as the source of authority.

### Recognition at PDP and Cart

This is the strongest direction after Pressure retreats. The Universal PDP uses a dependency chain rather than a conventional media-left/panel-right split. The Fitted PDP uses named endpoints, a visible direct break, a conditional bypass, and an unresolved segment. Cart becomes a consequence path terminating in totals and recovery. P0 red punctuation is removed; recognition survives through spacing, endpoints, witness rules, and relationship grammar.

### Improvement over Iteration 02

It transforms state anatomy from repeated panels into spatial logic. Compatibility and fit are no longer merely colored boxes, contributor states visibly feed the action, and Cart’s eight lines form one order-level path rather than a dashboard or summary sidebar. It is the clearest proof that Pressure & Proof can be both systematic and ownable.

### Weaknesses and gates

- The system can become engineering software if every fact is rendered as a node or connector.
- Plain-language summaries must always precede or accompany notation; the visual path cannot become a comprehension prerequisite.
- Real product photography and human editorial content are needed to add culture and material warmth.
- Relationship labels, path ordering, focus order, live announcements, and alternative text need assistive-technology testing.
- Verified relationship data, evidence ownership, stale-state operations, and recovery-service behavior are production prerequisites.

### Decision

**ADVANCE FOR USER CONSIDERATION — Rank 1.** It is the strongest complete-system hypothesis in this test, not an automatic selection. The user must choose whether its proprietary decision language best represents Blowin’ Smoke. If chosen, the next design phase should balance it with real photography and human voice before any token or implementation work begins.

## Grayscale and thumbnail result

All three remain distinguishable without their direction names:

- **A** reads as alternating poster blocks, oversized type, abrupt black fields, and short ledger sections.
- **B** reads as a continuous tall folio with numbered chapters, paper rhythm, captions, and contained record sheets.
- **C** reads as a dark routed opening followed by discrete path-based white/cream decision bands.

The 25% board preserves those silhouettes. The grayscale board confirms that differentiation is structural rather than a recolor exercise.

## Known Quick Cart issue outside this comparison

Quick Cart is not one of the five Iteration 03 surfaces. The eventual selected direction must place a concise highest-consequence issue summary **above the fixed action region** so **“View Full Cart” never visually precedes the reason review is required**. Iteration 02 remains unchanged during this study.

## Unresolved gates common to every direction

### Brand

- User selection among the three candidates.
- A supplied compact production mark, if one is ever needed; none was fabricated here.
- Final typeface selection, licensing, voice, and photography art direction.
- Confirmation that “Who Wants That Smoke?” retains challenge without creating coercion or violence cues in surrounding content.

### Product data and operations

- Real catalog, variant, batch, price, inventory, eligibility, compatibility, fit, contents, and relationship data.
- Manufacturer/maker sources, receiving inspection, verification ownership, review dates, stale rules, and conflict resolution.
- Real recovery routes, support handoff, cart revalidation, and fulfillment behavior.

### Photography and assets

- Decision-oriented product media, scale views, connection views, packaging/proof views, maker context, and missing-media fallbacks.
- Validation that real imagery strengthens—not neutralizes—the selected direction.

### Accessibility

- Screen-reader reading and relationship descriptions.
- Functional focus containment and restoration.
- Live announcements for selection, price, state, and cart changes.
- Keyboard operation, 200%/400% zoom, text-spacing overrides, localization, reflow, and cognitive-load testing.
- Reduced-motion equivalence and forced-colors verification in target browsers and assistive technologies.

## Research boundary

No direction has been converted into governing tokens. No system document, production application, runtime directory, catalog service, compatibility engine, or commerce implementation was changed or started. Selection and implementation remain unauthorized.
