## Shared semantic boundary

- Product truth outranks expression; pressure decreases with purchase consequence.
- Current Proof is documentary, not generic success.
- Unknown, incompatibility, restriction, service error, availability, eligibility, proof, and required-component states remain separate and labeled beyond color.
- Inline Status, Decision Summary, and Full Evidence remain distinct density roles.
- Blocked purchase/progression controls and enabled recovery controls remain separate.
- The observed brand red is never a semantic state color.
- P0 recognition must survive without the logo, a red field, decorative smoke, or display-scale typography.
