Article I's Decision Test and the master system's decision-quality test apply to every surface: the design must respect the customer, strengthen the independent house, and support a correct action without manufacturing certainty.

## A. Constructed Signal

Constructed Signal is reserved for:

- route;
- scope;
- dependency;
- exact endpoint;
- exception;
- consequence;
- recovery;
- terminal state.

It does not decorate ordinary facts. Routine node maps, flowchart styling, process dashboards, universal arrows, or codes around every fact are prohibited. This implements `docs/system/05-visual-design-system.md`, Sections 16, 19, and 26: relationship notation is useful only when endpoints, state, conditions, and consequence are explicit.

## B. Plain language first

A customer must understand the decision without decoding notation. A diagram, path, endpoint, or dependency graphic is secondary evidence and must have an equivalent text record. Technical language is introduced only when it helps a customer identify, compare, verify, or recover.

## C. Pressure distribution

- Home alone may use full Poster Pressure: one bounded near-black opening, the spacious unmodified logo, one narrow observed-red witness rule, one short display proposition, and immediate three-division orientation.
- The Vape & Nicotine opening may retain scale, asymmetry, directness, and strong pacing. It must not repeat Home's red punctuation as a division motif.
- Shared Category, Universal PDP, Fitted Component PDP, Quick Cart, Full Cart, errors, fit, support, and recovery remain free of campaign styling, oversized display copy, decorative smoke, and brand-red state treatment.
- Brand red is never compatibility, fit, restriction, error, proof, or purchase-readiness meaning.

This follows the Pressure-to-Proof continuum in `docs/system/05-visual-design-system.md`, Sections 2 and 7, and `docs/system/06-high-fidelity-page-design-specifications.md`, Section 4.

## D. Discovery order

Product identity precedes decision state. Resolved products remain visually quieter than records with a consequential blocker or material uncertainty. A promotion never displaces identity, a blocker, availability, price basis, or the next truthful action.

Product cards retain the shared semantic grammar in `docs/system/01-master-design-commerce-system.md`, Part V, and `docs/system/05-visual-design-system.md`, Section 20, while the rendered composition may vary by consequence.

## E. Visible decision order

The customer-facing order is immutable:

> Exact identity → selected state → price basis → availability → material blocker → reason → recovery → commerce action.

This visible sequence does not replace the readiness evaluation governed by `docs/system/01-master-design-commerce-system.md`, Part IV, and `docs/system/03-data-model-catalog-schema.md`, Part XXVII:

> Age qualification → destination eligibility → product-specific eligibility → availability → option completeness → compatibility → required components → material proof → price → purchase method → CTA readiness.

The composed evaluation supplies the highest blocker and the CTA. The page then presents that result in the fixed customer-facing sequence. CTA labels and enabled states are derived outputs, never independently authored merchandising fields.

## F. Unknown and adjacent states

Unknown, Unverified, Not Supplied, Not Applicable, Stale, Conflicting, Pending Verification, Loading, service Error, Incompatible, Restricted, Unavailable, and Changed remain distinct.

- Unknown never becomes a soft yes.
- A blank is not a semantic Unknown state.
- A failed eligibility service is an Error, not a Restricted outcome.
- In stock does not imply eligible, compatible, proven, or complete.
- Similar name, shared brand, appearance, image, review, or co-purchase does not establish compatibility or fit.
- Missing media does not remove textual identity or decision truth.

These distinctions follow `docs/system/01-master-design-commerce-system.md`, Parts XXII and XXIV, and `docs/system/03-data-model-catalog-schema.md`, Parts XXV–XXVII.

## G. Quick Cart

Quick Cart confirms and routes only. Its visible sequence remains:

> Line context → review boundary → highest-consequence issue → concise reason → actions.

Line context includes the exact selected state and current demo price basis where applicable. The drawer makes no checkout-readiness or full-order-validation claim, contains no recommendation rail, and keeps both actions visible. Complex or cross-line resolution routes to Full Cart with exact context preserved.

This boundary follows `docs/system/02-information-architecture-page-system.md`, Part XIX; `docs/system/04-page-by-page-architecture-specifications.md`, Contract 29; and `docs/system/06-high-fidelity-page-design-specifications.md`, Section 26.1.

## H. Full Cart

All eight fixture lines remain identifiable. Only the single highest-consequence conflict is expanded by default. Every collapsed secondary line retains:

- exact line identity;
- state label;
- concise reason;
- direct recovery.

The total basis, blocked progression, and recovery remain visible. The cart must read as a humane order review, not an admin dashboard, compliance checklist, Kanban board, or project tracker. The governing contract is `docs/system/04-page-by-page-architecture-specifications.md`, Contract 30.

## Customer-facing transformation lock

Visible surfaces do not expose design-team annotations, test methods, internal P0–P4 labels, media contracts, implementation notes, or repeated study warnings. Each surface carries one compact notice near the top:

> Fictional products, prices, inventory, relationships, and policies for design evaluation only.

All fictional records originate in `fixtures/customer-facing-catalog.json`, use identifiers beginning with `DEMO-`, and remain structurally realistic but non-production. The fixture may demonstrate states; it cannot establish product, price, proof, compatibility, eligibility, policy, or operational truth.

## Page-family locks

- **Home:** house proposition + three immediate division routes + human counter context → expanded division explanation → judgment standard → bounded authored curation → Learn → Support.
- **Vape & Nicotine:** “I want / I own / I need” precedes taxonomy; only one material device-to-component relationship appears in the opening journey; the condensed structured lifecycle guidance is present, while a separate platform-lifecycle media study remains a production-media boundary.
- **Shared Category:** category identity and active context precede controls and products; the principal grid remains five resolved, one caution, and two blockers; excess exception states stay in the study.
- **Universal PDP:** exact product and decision state read as a product page first; secondary records may disclose, but blockers never do.
- **Fitted Component PDP:** direct verdict, concise reason, meaningful supporting geometry, unresolved clearance, adapter route, blocked action, and measurement recovery remain visible before deeper evidence.
- **Quick Cart:** an existing line is revalidated after owned context changes; it never claims the blocked Fitted PDP action succeeded. Viewport containment and reason-before-actions remain fixed.
- **Full Cart:** eight lines, one expanded conflict, seven individually recoverable secondary issues.

Page responsibilities are governed by `docs/system/04-page-by-page-architecture-specifications.md`, Contracts 02, 05, 07, 13, 21, 29, and 30.

## Implementation boundary

This package may use semantic HTML, package-local CSS, package-local fictional JSON, native disclosure, and neutral non-SKU media studies. It does not authorize production components, catalog or relationship logic, eligibility rules, checkout, search services, accounts, support cases, inventory, payment, or application integration. Provisional visual values remain test evidence rather than production tokens.
