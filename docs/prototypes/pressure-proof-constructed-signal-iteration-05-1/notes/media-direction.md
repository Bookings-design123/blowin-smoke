# Iteration 05.1 Media Direction

**Status:** Internal media contract for a static, non-production prototype  
**Implementation authorization:** Not granted

## Governing boundary

Media is evidence before atmosphere. It may answer recognition, scale, connection, contents, assembly, material, or context questions, but it cannot be the sole source of a critical fact. The governing sources are `docs/system/01-master-design-commerce-system.md`, Part XII; `docs/system/03-data-model-catalog-schema.md`, Part XXI; `docs/system/05-visual-design-system.md`, Sections 17–18; and `docs/system/06-high-fidelity-page-design-specifications.md`, Section 6.8.

Iteration 05.1 does not use public product images, competitor assets, competitor packaging, third-party stock photography, or fabricated product photography. Image-ready frames and original package-local neutral CSS studies demonstrate crop, hierarchy, and media rhythm. A neutral study must not resemble a real product or package closely enough to imply an actual SKU.

All aspect ratios below are provisional composition hypotheses, not production tokens.

## Shared media rules

- Assign every media position a customer decision job.
- Keep exact identity and critical facts understandable without the asset.
- Use structured text for price, option, availability, contents, specifications, compatibility, fit, eligibility, and recovery.
- Do not infer product truth from a crop, filename, illustration, silhouette, or apparently similar object.
- Preserve connector, joint, control, label, measurement reference, and logo clear space at every viewport.
- Missing media uses an honest identity placeholder, never a neighboring product.
- Alt text describes the assigned media job and visible subject; it does not hide specifications or repeat adjacent text.
- Decorative geometry receives an empty alternative.
- No image may prove compatibility or physical fit.

## Production-media gates

This package can test frame geometry, rhythm, crop intent, media absence, alt-text intent, and the relationship between media and structured truth. It cannot establish final visual credibility without:

- approved original photography or product media;
- exact product/variant assignments;
- verified media permissions and provenance;
- real crop review at all responsive sizes;
- authoritative contents, scale, connection, and measurement truth;
- final alt text written against the actual asset and adjacent content;
- accessibility and device testing of galleries or media controls.

No prototype frame or neutral study should be promoted into production by default.

The current Vape page supplies the complete lifecycle guidance in structured text. It does not include a separate lifecycle media sequence and therefore does not claim that such a production asset system has been proven.
