# Iteration 05.1 Home Anti-Generic Audit

**Result:** CONDITIONAL PASS for static direction; production media remains a gate.

## Final opening correction

Iteration 05's black split hero still risked reading as a generic streetwear or AI-agency landing page. Iteration 05.1 keeps one bounded Pressure field but changes the composition, not merely the accent:

- headline ceiling: 86 CSS px wide and 52 CSS px compact;
- one narrow witness rule replaces the generic red dot;
- THCA, Vape & Nicotine, and Glass & Accessories sit inside the opening decision sequence at wide, 390, and 320;
- the unmodified padded logo remains one spacious editorial house mark;
- an abstract two-figure counter study assigns a specific product-handling and listening role to future approved media without claiming that the current drawing proves that interaction;
- the transition into the fuller route explanation is visible at the wide viewport boundary;
- the same division architecture survives grayscale and logo-de-emphasized inspection.

## Brand-reduced test

With the logo area visually de-emphasized, the opening still communicates three named divisions, a customer route, and an independent selection standard. Distinctiveness therefore does not rely on logo recognition alone.

## Grayscale test

Removing color retains asymmetry, route order, type hierarchy, the counter relationship, and the transition into retail content. Observed red is punctuation, not the sole carrier of meaning.

## Remaining production boundary

The CSS counter study proves only that the composition reserves a media role. It does not visually prove listening, product handling, operator identity, or a real customer interaction. Real, approved operator/environment media must make those intended qualities legible without inventing biography, endorsement, or product truth.
