## Anti-generic judgment

The final Home no longer depends only on “large white slogan on black plus CTA.” Retail architecture is part of the opening, not delayed to a later section. The abstract counter study allocates a practical future-media role, while the unmodified logo remains a house mark rather than a copied commerce device. Color removal retains route order, asymmetry, and retail meaning. Brand-reduced inspection retains the three-division structure.

The direction still requires real, properly sourced media and a production typography decision before launch. That boundary does not block the static gate; it blocks production approval.

## Overall decision

**CONDITIONAL PASS.** Iteration 05.1 closes the required static architecture correction gate. It may serve as the customer-facing prototype direction for subsequent separately authorized content, media, interaction, accessibility, data, and engineering work. It is not a production approval.

**Implementation remains unauthorized.**
