# Blowin’ Smoke — Pressure & Proof Art Direction Iteration 04

## Constructed Signal, Humanized

**Status:** Synthetic static art-direction evidence; non-production

**Implementation authorization:** Not granted

This isolated package tests whether Constructed Signal can remain a proprietary Blowin’ Smoke decision language while becoming more human, product-led, culturally credible, and desirable. Direction C from Iteration 03 supplies the structural grammar; it is not accepted unchanged as a final visual design.

Iteration 04 preserves route spines, scope markers, named endpoints, dependency order, direct breaks, conditional paths, unknown segments, contributor logic, consequence-to-recovery paths, and terminal totals. It removes routine node coding, dashboard-like equal modules, notation that precedes plain language, and relationship diagrams applied to facts that do not change a decision.

## Controlled visual influence

- **Constructed Signal governs structure:** route, scope, dependency, endpoint, exception, recovery, and terminal logic.
- **Poster Pressure enters only upstream:** Home uses controlled asymmetry, confident scale, abrupt pacing, direct challenge-led copy, and one restrained brand-red punctuation device. The division keeps scale, asymmetry, and directness but deliberately removes red.
- **Independent Archive does not become a skin:** no folios, fake heritage, accession language, or routine archival styling is used. No maker/provenance module is included because the fixed synthetic subjects do not establish a genuine maker claim.
- **P0 remains recognizable without borrowed expression:** Universal PDP, Fitted PDP, Quick Cart, and Full Cart use no logo, brand-red field, campaign copy, decorative smoke, or display-scale typography.

## Humanization contract

Humanization comes from composition, product prominence, direct language, and purpose-specific media positions—not decorative people, invented stories, or cultural costume.

| Surface | Synthetic media position | Exact future job | If media is absent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Spacious logo plus operator/environment study | Establish independent human presence and house memory; never substantiate a product or cultural claim | House promise, three divisions, judgment standard, and routes remain complete in text |
| Vape & Nicotine | Device-in-hand/context study and platform-family product study | Orient “I want / I own / I need,” distinguish lifecycle roles, and show where exact identity matters; not compatibility proof | Job routes, named roles, and relationship statements remain complete |
| Shared Category | Product identity, form, and scale studies | Reduce misrecognition and support comparison; never establish fit, proof, or eligibility | Exact identity, decision facts, state, and recovery remain visible |
| Universal PDP | Exact identity, selected-option, and contents studies | Confirm the object and configuration cues; not authoritative catalog evidence | Identity, selected state, price state, availability, blocker, recovery, and action remain complete |
| Fitted Component PDP | Source/target connector, orientation, and measurement studies | Support endpoint recognition and measurement recovery; not compatibility proof until verified | All endpoint, geometry, evidence, consequence, and recovery claims remain textual |
| Quick Cart | Exact line thumbnail | Confirm the line and selected option only | Exact line identity, issue, reason, and route remain complete |
| Full Cart | Exact line thumbnails | Help recognize eight selected lines; not validate any relationship | All eight identities, states, highest conflict, totals basis, and recoveries remain complete |

Every media position is visibly labeled as a synthetic study. No product, person, maker, customer, environment, provenance, price, inventory, proof, eligibility, compatibility, fit, or policy claim is represented as real.
