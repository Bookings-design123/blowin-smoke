# Blowin’ Smoke — Constructed Signal, Humanized Evaluation

**Status:** PASS as static art-direction evidence; not production approval

**Implementation authorization:** Not granted

**Evaluation date:** 2026-08-11

## Decision under test

Direction C from Iteration 03 is selected as the governing structural grammar for this study, not as a finished visual design. Iteration 04 asks whether Blowin’ Smoke can retain visible decision intelligence while becoming more human, product-led, culturally credible, and desirable.

The answer is **yes, with a strict operating rule**: Constructed Signal should describe routes, dependencies, exceptions, consequences, recovery, and terminal states only when those relationships change a customer decision. It should not decorate ordinary facts. Poster Pressure can establish the house upstream, but must withdraw before configuration, fit, cart, error, and support decisions.

This conclusion is based on seven linked synthetic surfaces, 21 responsive surface renders, ten Iteration 03 C comparison boards, two all-surface boards, browser-derived measurements, and individual visual inspection of every committed PNG.

## Evaluation basis and limits

The study was evaluated against Article I, Systems 01–06 and 08, Iteration 03’s selected direction, the required humanization contract, and the anti-generic audit. All identities, options, prices, states, media, evidence references, and cart lines are visibly synthetic.

The prototype proves hierarchy and art direction. It does not prove runtime behavior, catalog truth, legal readiness, assistive-technology behavior, checkout readiness, or production accessibility. The detailed rendering record is in `notes/rendering-evidence.json`.

## Typography, color, contrast, and responsive evidence

### Horizontal overflow and responsive hierarchy

All seven surfaces were measured at 1440 × 900, 390 × 844, and 320 × 760: **21 of 21 reported no horizontal document overflow**. Additional checks at 1024 and 901 CSS pixels exposed an early grid risk; the responsive threshold was revised and both widths then passed without overflow.

The wide header retains the primary division navigation. At 390 and 320, that row yields to the compact house and utility structure; the linked prototype remains navigable through the page routes and index. No capture showed clipped essential content or a corrupted stitch.

### Typography actually rendered

The prototype uses installed system faces only; no font is downloaded or committed.

### Control-height hypotheses

Key button-like actions measured at least 44 CSS pixels in all 21 target renders; Quick Cart’s action region uses 48-pixel controls. This supports the art-direction hypothesis for prominent actions. It does not prove that every inline text link meets a production touch-target requirement, nor does it replace keyboard, switch, zoom, or mobile-device testing.

## Final visual-inspection record

Every one of the **33 committed PNGs** was opened and visually inspected after its last material change:

- 7 wide surface renders;
- 7 narrow surface renders;
- 7 compact 320-wide surface renders;
- 5 wide Iteration 03 C comparisons;
- 5 narrow Iteration 03 C comparisons;
- 1 grayscale hierarchy board;
- 1 approximately 25% opening-thumbnail board.

Inspection found no blank or truncated surface, broken local media, unintended horizontal crop, repeated capture segment, missing Quick Cart action region, or illegible comparison label. Wide captures were assembled from browser viewport frames because the capture service returned a narrower physical bitmap than the requested CSS viewport; frames were normalized to the verified 1440 CSS-pixel width before stitching. Quick Cart used a viewport-contained composite so the right-anchored drawer edge and close control remained exact.

Humanization improved most clearly in the Home’s house moment, the division’s job-first opening, the category’s product-led result rows, and the PDPs’ media-supported plain-language decisions. Constructed Signal became too schematic when ordinary facts were treated as nodes and, during iteration, when the Fitted recovery rows compressed prose into narrow columns. Both conditions were removed. Poster Pressure remains visible on Home through scale, asymmetry, challenge-led copy, abrupt pacing, and one red punctuation; the division retains scale, asymmetry, and directness without red. It is deliberately absent from Category, PDP, Quick Cart, Full Cart, error, and support decisions.

## Accessibility provisions in the static evidence

The sources include a skip link, semantic header/main/footer regions, one `h1` per page, visible focus treatment, explicit disabled styling, reduced-motion handling, forced-colors adjustments, text-first decisions, and status language that does not depend on color alone. The study contains no scripted motion. Local links, fragment references, IDs, and ARIA references passed static validation.

These provisions are design evidence, not an accessibility certification.

## Unresolved gates

The following remain open and must not be inferred from this package:

1. Production photography, product imagery, copy, prices, inventory, proof, eligibility, compatibility, fit, maker, customer, and policy truth are absent by design.
2. Final typography and production tokens are unapproved; the tested system-font fallback is evidence only.
3. Quick Cart focus trapping, focus return, inert background behavior, escape handling, and scroll restoration are not implemented or tested.
4. Keyboard, screen-reader, switch-control, browser zoom/reflow, real-device touch, localization, and content-expansion testing remain required.
5. The responsive evidence covers the stated viewports plus targeted 901/1024 checks, not a production browser/device matrix.
6. Contrast was calculated for declared pairs; every eventual component state and non-text boundary still requires production validation.
7. Real catalog relationships, measurement standards, age/eligibility rules, legal policy, tax, shipping, totals, and checkout behavior require authoritative data and implementation review.
8. Static links and disabled controls do not demonstrate functional selection, search, cart, recovery, or support behavior.

## Research boundary

No production tokens, application components, runtime services, catalog data, compatibility engine, cart behavior, or implementation were created or authorized. Iterations 01–03 and governing system documents remain unchanged. **Implementation remains unauthorized.**
