# Iteration 05 Anti-Generic Audit

**Status:** Completed static-render audit; non-production  
**Implementation authorization:** Not granted

## Standard

Distinctiveness must come from composition, pacing, route structure, selected product roles, exact decision order, house voice, asymmetric staging, meaningful media, and useful recovery. It must not be manufactured by adding more black, red, borders, codes, or oversized type.

The audit follows the refusals in `docs/constitution/01-brand-philosophy.md`, the visual anti-patterns in `docs/system/05-visual-design-system.md`, Section 37, and the Iteration 05 anti-generic requirements. No more than two consecutive sections may use the same repeated equal-column composition.

The findings below evaluate the current Iteration 05 pages and committed render set. “Pass” means the specific resemblance was not material in the inspected static evidence; it does not mean production readiness. “Watch” identifies a resemblance that remains plausible when final media, longer live content, or interaction is introduced.

## Governing conclusion

Iteration 05 materially removes the annotated-research and engineering-software character of Iteration 04 while retaining the exact decision signature. The remaining visual risks are concentrated, not hidden: Home can still drift into generic bold streetwear, Universal PDP still uses a familiar retail skeleton, and Fitted PDP will always sit near a technical-density boundary. Those are conditional art-direction gates for real media, final typography, live content, and implemented interaction.

This audit supports static design evaluation only. It does not grant production implementation, accessibility certification, media approval, catalog truth, or legal/commerce readiness. **Implementation remains unauthorized.**
