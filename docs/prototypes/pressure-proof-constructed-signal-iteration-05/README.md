## Purpose

Iteration 05 asks whether the fixed Iteration 04 Constructed Signal grammar can read as an original, desirable storefront instead of an annotated research artifact. It does not reopen the art direction, invent production catalog truth, or authorize implementation.

The tested proposition is:

> Blowin' Smoke can combine independent house character, customer-facing retail rhythm, precise product identity, explicit uncertainty, and useful recovery without imitating a competitor or turning commerce into engineering software.

The package keeps the governing decision sequence fixed:

> Exact identity → selected state → price basis → availability → material blocker → reason → recovery → commerce action.

## Fictional-data boundary

Every product, price, inventory value, relationship, compatibility result, physical-fit result, policy, and cart state in this package is fictional and exists only for design evaluation. Fixture identifiers begin with `DEMO-`. Each customer-facing surface carries exactly one compact notice:

> Fictional products, prices, inventory, relationships, and policies for design evaluation only.

The fixture deliberately stresses long and short identities, variants, quantities, package contents, required components, owned-device context, exact endpoints, compatibility and fit states, missing media, changed price, unavailable inventory, restriction, service error, stale evidence, unknown evidence, and eight cart lines. None of it establishes real catalog, legal, policy, proof, price, availability, compatibility, provenance, warranty, or performance truth.

## Frozen design decisions

- Plain language must communicate the decision without requiring a diagram.
- Constructed Signal notation is reserved for route, scope, dependency, endpoint, exception, consequence, recovery, and terminal state.
- Home alone receives full Poster Pressure. The division opening may retain scale and directness; decision, product, fit, cart, error, and support surfaces remain quieter.
- Product identity precedes state in discovery.
- Unknown, unverified, stale, error, incompatible, restricted, unavailable, and changed remain distinct.
- Unknown is never a soft yes.
- A blocked commerce action and an enabled recovery action remain separate.
- Quick Cart confirms and routes; it does not validate the full order or recommend products.
- Full Cart keeps all eight lines identifiable and expands only the highest-consequence conflict by default.

The detailed lock is in `notes/decision-lock.md`.

## Customer-facing transformation

Iteration 05 moves design-team rationale, P-level labels, media contracts, test methods, and implementation commentary out of the visible storefront and into package notes. Customer-facing copy is direct, concise, and literal where a decision is consequential. "Who wants that smoke?" appears once, in the Home opening.

This transformation preserves prototype honesty through the single notice, DEMO fixture identifiers, and source metadata rather than repeated warning copy. `notes/customer-facing-copy-contract.md` records the governing language rules.

## Media-integration boundary

The package uses image-ready positions and original neutral studies. It does not use competitor assets, public product imagery, stock photography, invented maker history, or fabricated SKU photography. Media can support recognition, scale, contents, connection, or context, but it cannot establish compatibility, fit, provenance, safety, availability, or eligibility.

The neutral studies demonstrate composition and information priority only. Final customer credibility, crop quality, material detail, object recognition, and house specificity cannot be approved without properly sourced production media. The complete position-by-position contract is in `notes/media-direction.md`.

## Typography decision

Candidate A—the declared `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` system stack—is selected provisionally for these seven static surfaces. It best separates assertive upstream roles from upright product, technical, cart, and recovery roles without tracing the logo or importing a font asset.

The evidence environment reported the declared candidate stack as the computed family string and recorded the expected role weights. That result is environment-specific. No font was downloaded or committed, and the selection is not a licensed, cross-platform, glyph-complete, zoom-tested, or production-approved typography decision. The audition and its rejection rationale for Candidates B and C are in `notes/typography-audition.md`.

## Responsive, motion, and accessibility notes

- The package uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, one `h1` per surface, native links/controls, native `<details>/<summary>`, visible focus treatment, explicit state language, and no color-only decision state.
- Prominent controls use a minimum 44-pixel target hypothesis; final measurements found no tested control below 44 CSS pixels.
- Package CSS includes reduced-motion and forced-colors treatments.
- The customer-facing pages contain no JavaScript. Static states therefore do not claim animated choreography, drawer topology, focus control, live state transitions, or production keyboard behavior.
- Reduced motion must preserve the same identity, state, reason, recovery, and action. Motion may never be the only carrier of meaning.
- Long product identities and current-page labels are represented in committed evidence. Required 1440, 1024, 901, 390, and 320 checks all returned `scrollWidth == clientWidth`.

This is not screen-reader certification, zoom certification, touch-device certification, production keyboard certification, or WCAG conformance approval.

## Anti-generic result

The static compositions remove the dominant annotated-research and engineering-software character of Iteration 04. Distinctiveness comes primarily from route order, pressure distribution, consequence-led density, exact endpoint language, authored curation, asymmetry, and recovery—not from red repetition, smoke graphics, pill stacks, glass effects, discount theater, or a generic card grid.

Residual risks remain explicit:

- Home can drift toward a generic bold streetwear shell without specific production media and final type;
- Universal PDP uses a familiar gallery/purchase-panel skeleton and therefore depends on authored content, decision order, and media;
- Fitted PDP must keep its technical evidence subordinate to the first-screen answer;
- category and cart density can regress into dashboard styling when live data arrives.

The complete resemblance ledger and corrections are in `notes/anti-generic-audit.md`.

## Stop conditions

Stop review, refuse a pass, and do not advance this work if any of the following is true:

1. A required source, render, viewport, state, board, validation result, or visual-inspection result is missing or stale after a material change.
2. A PNG is blank, repeated, corrupt, horizontally cropped, missing navigation, missing an action region, or unreadable at its intended review scale.
3. Product identity no longer precedes state, or the fixed decision order changes.
4. Unknown, unverified, stale, error, incompatible, restricted, unavailable, or changed is collapsed into another state or implied by color alone.
5. A critical verdict, blocker, reason, recovery, selected state, total basis, or commerce consequence is hidden by disclosure.
6. Quick Cart cannot show its highest issue, reason, and both actions in the tested viewport geometry.
7. Full Cart loses any of the eight lines or expands more than the highest-consequence conflict by default.
8. Search, Cart, Menu, current-page context, or any primary route disappears at compact widths without a named replacement.
9. Media is treated as compatibility, fit, proof, eligibility, availability, or provenance evidence.
10. A declared typeface, weight, contrast pair, control size, link, ID, ARIA reference, or responsive result is accepted without current measurement.
11. Internal research commentary, duplicated study notices, copied trade dress, competitor assets, stock photography, fabricated claims, or unsupported urgency appears.
12. Work crosses into application code, production catalog logic, eligibility, compatibility, fit, inventory, checkout, payment, support, account, or legal implementation.
