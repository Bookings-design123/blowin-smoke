## 1. Evidence method

- Authoritative Apple documentation, public SDK declarations, Apple Developer Technical Support, Apple platform guidance, Telegram first-party documentation, and Telegram's official issue tracker were opened and inspected. Search-result text was used only to locate sources, never as the sole basis for a material conclusion.
- `WHAT IT ESTABLISHES` is the maximum supported claim. `WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH` is a mandatory boundary.
- A documented API is not execution proof. A shipping third-party behavior is not proof of its mechanism. A local header is not proof that a device compositor honors the declaration.
- The owner observation is retained as `OWNER-OBSERVED REFERENCE EVIDENCE`. It is neither fabricated test evidence nor an attribution to Telegram internals.
- Absence of a qualifying public contract is `BLOCKED — EVIDENCE NOT ESTABLISHED`, not a universal impossibility claim.
- Capture acceptance is artifact-based: a supported screenshot or recording passes only when the complete protected surface contains zero readable protected information. One readable recording frame fails that tested path.

## 6. Evidence disposition

The evidence materially narrows, but does not close, the SEC-03 iOS gap:

- Apple still does not expose a supported general arbitrary UIKit/SwiftUI screenshot veto.
- Apple does expose a supported capture-protected sample-buffer layer, and the layer accepts uncompressed frames. A complete rasterized protected-pixel plane is therefore a legitimate public-API proof hypothesis.
- The public contract does not establish complete application behavior, overlay inheritance, physical screenshot/recording results, first-frame output, accessibility, external-output behavior, or operational viability.
- Telegram corroborates the desired product behavior and the possibility of black output, while its official evidence also demonstrates nonuniform iOS enforcement. Telegram does not establish the mechanism.
- Secure-text arbitrary-view techniques cannot substitute for the public sample-buffer proof path.

**Primary unmanaged iOS/iPadOS status:** `CONDITIONAL — APP-STORE-VIABLE TECHNIQUE REQUIRES DEVICE PROOF`.

This is not `PROVEN CANDIDATE`, not production approval, and not a finding that Blowin' Smoke itself is App Store eligible. It means only that a public-API rendering hypothesis is concrete enough to justify a physical proof. Overall tobacco/vape commerce distribution remains a separate policy/legal gate.
